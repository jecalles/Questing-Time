**Human · she/her · 15 · Outlaw's Daughter — "Ember"**

**Player:** Lily

> _It has to be something, because it can't be nothing. We are not doing nothing anymore._

A fifteen-year-old human, underfed and pale from seven years asleep, in a one-piece worker's coverall, the trousers baggy and cinched at the ankle over boots, a white tee under it, and a thigh holster for her weapon, tools and reagents, everything oversized, workwear cut the Japanese way; her hair grown to her ankles and worn in one long braid. Undercover, she wears whatever uniform the job needs. A work hammer too big for her, a multi-tool on her belt, a bag of tools with a manifesto in it. She slips out of rooms as a first instinct and camouflages into any company as a second. Something follows her that is most visible in shadow. The night she ran with [Ada](../NPC%20Sheets/Ada%20Crane.md)'s books, forest-green fatigues.

**Now:** Health **Normal** · Tokens not given at Session Six · with the party in [the schoolhouse library](../Lore/Places/The%20Schoolhouse%20Library.md), before dawn · as of Session Six (2026-10-07). Log: [Ember Spanner](../Character%20Logs/Ember%20Spanner.md).

## ABILITIES

**Rebellious** — Authority does not land on her the way it's supposed to. No mechanic yet; one gets written when play calls for it.

**Shy** — disadvantage on Charisma checks against anyone she is **Misaligned** with. Unfamiliarity, not timidity: fluent with her own people, lost with everyone else. It lifts as she earns standing. Mechanically it keys off Alignment, so it lifts as standing is earned rather than at the GM's discretion, and it starts near-total: every character begins Misaligned with every faction. This is what a d20 Charisma costs her. The stat is her magic and her camouflage; it is not a licence to talk anyone into anything. Designed jointly with the player.

**Evasive** — advantage on reactive checks to avoid damage. A lifetime of leaving rooms before anyone noticed, turned into a habit the body keeps. She can leave a melee zone without the Prowess check, as a rogue does. The Shade shares it, and the two upgrade together, along Shade Advancement.

**Tinkering** — traps only, for now. Given sufficient material components, she can build common items: simple traps, simple weapons, and comparable workshop goods. Planned Actions only; this needs hands, tools and time. Traps may be built to type — smoke, explosive, frost, poison — where she has the components for it. Magical traps carry a higher DC than mundane ones, and those DCs ease as her tinkering improves. Acquired, not innate: the GM grants it after a short narrative beat of her remembering the workshop she grew up in, roughly five minutes at the table, and eligible to happen during Session Zero. It supersedes the narrower Skilled at Mending it grew out of.

**Spark** — Minor, Gifted, Evocation. A spark at her fingers: no roll to light a fuse, a lamp, kindling or a charge she set. Against a person, Charisma against their Instinct.
- **Upgrades.** Lesser reaches a point she can see within one zone: it lights a set charge, or flashes in a face (disadvantage on that target's next roll). Common reaches about 120 ft, or throws a short cone of fire for one Health step. Making fire that is already there go off bigger is Greater, and comes only through her patron. See [Reach by level](../Lore/Magic%20System.md#reach-by-level).

**Peripheral Sight** — she perceives what is watching — presence, intent, things past the party's vision — and must describe it **while looking somewhere else.** If she turns to look directly, it's gone. For reactive perception she rolls **Charisma in place of Instinct**, but only while looking away. _Lost entirely while Drowsy._

**The Drowse** _(flaw)_ — she is running on something that runs out.
- **Damage.** Each damage level she takes past Minor calls a luck check — Instinct (d6): one at Major, one at Critical. Fail → she goes Under.
- **Exhaustion.** Holding out against it — no sleep, a long march, a night on watch → Constitution check (d4). Fail → gain 1 Drowse (and a Determination Token).
- Pushing a Shade power costs nothing.
- **3 Drowse — Drowsy:** disadvantage on anything requiring alertness; loses Peripheral Sight. **5 Drowse — Under:** she drops, incapacitated for the scene, unwakeable.
- A long rest clears 2, a short rest 1; **being genuinely reached clears 1** — someone using her name, meeting her eye, an honest gesture of care. She should hate that this works.
- _Later upgrade:_ while Under, she heals one damage level per turn.
- If the Shade is knocked out, she goes Under at once.

### The Shade

_Not a piece of her. It wears the shape of the shadow it took from her, and to her it feels like loneliness itself._ **Idle:** watches from the shadows. **Visibility:** most visible in shadow; incorporeal in normal light; forced to the nearest shadow by powerful light. **Manner:** it works by inference, not instruction. Ember has rarely asked it for anything, so it acts on what it has seen her do and what it knows of her mother. Told only "help," it does the most Shade-like thing available. The player's model for it is No-Face from *Spirited Away*.

**Stat block:** it shares hers. When it rolls, it rolls her die for that stat. It shares her **Evasive** too.

- **In darkness:** it has advantage.
- **Against light magic:** it is vulnerable, to clerics most of all.
- **If it is knocked out:** Ember goes Under at once.

_All Shade powers are Gifted magic: Charisma against the target's Instinct._

**Mirror Manifestation** — a shadowy mirror of Ember: distraction, hallucination, a spook, or a scout as long as there is ample shadow. In combat it has Ember's basic abilities and her Evasive — but any damage done to it is also done to Ember.

**Damage sharing is intended, not a hole** — it is the deliberate cost of a companion that can, at higher tiers, dodge or ghost a hit entirely. Base state: any damage done to the Shade is also done to Ember. **Sharing survives the Goad.** Pulling attacks onto a damage-sharing companion looks self-defeating, and it is not: the Shade is harder to hit than Ember, because it shares Evasive and that shared evasion upgrades over play. The Goad trades a certain hit on a d4 Constitution for a contested one against advantage. See Shade Advancement.

**Goad** — the Shade makes itself the obvious threat. Charisma against the target's Instinct; on a success, the target's next attack goes at the Shade instead. Her aggro tool, and why she can stand on a front line at all.

**Shade Attack** — it attacks in its own right, rolling her Prowess.

**Paralysis** — one target cannot move or push the air out to shout. Charisma against their Instinct. Brief.

**Transposition / Switch Places** (higher level) — she and the Shade trade positions, across zones. _Proposed: costs her action, not her move._ The Shade has already done this once, unprompted — it moved Ember into shadow to escape pursuit in [New Squishnesta](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md) before she had any idea it could. Treat that as a preview of this power, not a separate ability.

**At rest:** she can talk to it. It is never wrong. It is never kind.

## ITEMS AND EQUIPMENT

**Mother's Favorite** — a work hammer from her mother's shop. Adds her Charisma die to the attack pool alongside Prowess: she cannot throw a punch, she swings with everything she feels. Blunt. See Mother's Favorite.

**Second Hand** (formerly Jack) — a multi-tool, a gift from her mother on her tenth birthday: blade, picks, prybar, driver, cutter, and a fold-out lamp that does not work. A dagger rolled as a plain Prowess attack; artificer's tools that pick locks. A Key Item: older than it should be, with implements she hasn't identified; new functions unlock on story beats. She could fix the lamp. She hasn't. **First tier:** flint and steel, a fuse crimp and copper wire, enough to set and light a charge. See Second Hand.

**Her mother's book** — weathered, red letters on the cover, her mother's notes in the margins. Taken, not stolen. Bookmarked with a photograph of her mother and her younger self. A Key Item: she took it looking for clues to where her mother went, and she still reads it that way.

**Recall Stone** — **Spent.** A one-time escape. It takes you wherever its setter fixed it. Now it is a pretty engraved rock.

**Armour:** none. A light leather chest plate is her first armour upgrade, when she earns it.
