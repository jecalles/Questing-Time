**Human · she/her · 15 · Outlaw's Daughter — "Ember"**

**Player:** Lily

> _It has to be something, because it can't be nothing. We are not doing nothing anymore._

A fifteen-year-old human, underfed and pale from seven years asleep, in a one-piece worker's coverall, the trousers baggy and cinched at the ankle over boots, a white tee under it, and a thigh holster for her weapon, tools and reagents, everything oversized, workwear cut the Japanese way; her hair grown to her ankles and worn in one long braid. Undercover, she wears whatever uniform the job needs. A work hammer too big for her, a multi-tool on her belt, a bag of tools with a manifesto in it. She slips out of rooms as a first instinct and camouflages into any company as a second. Something follows her that is most visible in shadow. The night she ran with Ada's books, forest-green fatigues.

**Now:** Health **Normal** · Tokens not given at Session Six · with the party in the schoolhouse library, before dawn · as of Session Six (2026-10-07). Log: [Ember Spanner](../Character%20Logs/Ember%20Spanner.md).

## ABILITIES

**Rebellious** — Authority does not land on her the way it's supposed to. See [Rebellious](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#rebellious).

**Shy** — disadvantage on Charisma checks against anyone she is **Misaligned** with. Unfamiliarity, not timidity: fluent with her own people, lost with everyone else. It lifts as she earns standing. See [Shy](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#shy).

**Evasive** — advantage on reactive checks to avoid damage. She can leave a melee zone without the Prowess check, as a rogue does. The Shade shares it, and the two upgrade together. See [Evasive](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#evasive).

**Tinkering** — traps only, for now. Given the parts, she builds simple traps to type: smoke, explosive. Planned Actions only. See [Tinkering](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#tinkering).

**Spark** — Minor, Gifted, Evocation. A spark at her fingers: no roll to light a fuse, a lamp, kindling or a charge she set. Against a person, Charisma against their Instinct. See [Spark](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#spark).

**Peripheral Sight** — she perceives what is watching — presence, intent, things past the party's vision — and must describe it **while looking somewhere else.** If she turns to look directly, it's gone. For reactive perception she rolls **Charisma in place of Instinct**, but only while looking away. _Lost entirely while Drowsy._ See [Peripheral Sight](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#peripheral-sight).

**The Drowse** _(flaw)_ — she is running on something that runs out.
- **Damage.** Each damage level she takes past Minor calls a luck check — Instinct (d6): one at Major, one at Critical. Fail → she goes Under.
- **Exhaustion.** Holding out against it — no sleep, a long march, a night on watch → Constitution check (d4). Fail → gain 1 Drowse (and a Determination Token).
- Pushing a Shade power costs nothing.
- **3 Drowse — Drowsy:** disadvantage on anything requiring alertness; loses Peripheral Sight. **5 Drowse — Under:** she drops, incapacitated for the scene, unwakeable.
- A long rest clears 2, a short rest 1; **being genuinely reached clears 1** — someone using her name, meeting her eye, an honest gesture of care. She should hate that this works.
- _Later upgrade:_ while Under, she heals one damage level per turn.

See [The Drowse](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#the-drowse).

### The Shade

_Not a piece of her. It wears the shape of the shadow it took from her, and to her it feels like loneliness itself._ **Idle:** watches from the shadows. **Visibility:** most visible in shadow; incorporeal in normal light; forced to the nearest shadow by powerful light. **Manner:** it works by inference, not instruction — told only "help," it does the most Shade-like thing available.

**Stat block:** it shares hers. When it rolls, it rolls her die for that stat. It shares her **Evasive** too.

- **In darkness:** it has advantage.
- **Against light magic:** it is vulnerable, to clerics most of all.
- **If it is knocked out:** Ember goes Under at once.

_All Shade powers are Gifted magic: Charisma against the target's Instinct._

**Mirror Manifestation** — a shadowy mirror of Ember: distraction, hallucination, a spook, or a scout as long as there is ample shadow. In combat it has Ember's basic abilities and her Evasive — but any damage done to it is also done to Ember.

**Goad** — the Shade makes itself the obvious threat. Charisma against the target's Instinct; on a success, the target's next attack goes at the Shade instead. Her aggro tool, and why she can stand on a front line at all.

**Shade Attack** — it attacks in its own right, rolling her Prowess.

**Paralysis** — one target cannot move or push the air out to shout. Charisma against their Instinct. Brief.

**Transposition / Switch Places** (higher level) — she and the Shade trade positions, across zones. _Proposed: costs her action, not her move._

**At rest:** she can talk to it. It is never wrong. It is never kind.

Full entries: [The Shade](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#the-shade).

## ITEMS AND EQUIPMENT

**Mother's Favorite** — a work hammer from her mother's shop. Adds her Charisma die to the attack pool alongside Prowess: she cannot throw a punch, she swings with everything she feels. Blunt. See Mother's Favorite.

**Second Hand** (formerly Jack) — a multi-tool, a gift from her mother on her tenth birthday: blade, picks, prybar, driver, cutter, and a fold-out lamp that does not work. A dagger rolled as a plain Prowess attack; artificer's tools that pick locks. A Key Item: older than it should be, with implements she hasn't identified; new functions unlock on story beats. She could fix the lamp. She hasn't. **First tier:** flint and steel, a fuse crimp and copper wire, enough to set and light a charge. See Second Hand.

**Her mother's book** — weathered, red letters on the cover, her mother's notes in the margins. Taken, not stolen. Bookmarked with a photograph of her mother and her younger self. A Key Item: she took it looking for clues to where her mother went, and she still reads it that way.

**Recall Stone** — **Spent.** A one-time escape. It takes you wherever its setter fixed it. Now it is a pretty engraved rock.

**Armour:** none. A light leather chest plate is her first armour upgrade, when she earns it.
