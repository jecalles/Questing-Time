**Drau · she/her · Age 50 (young for a Drau) · Spore Druid — table name "Bad Trip"**

**Player:** Aera

> "Oh, darling, that's ridiculous. Can't you give us a moment of your time to explain?"

A dark elf of about fifty, which is young, in a long white dress cut like Victorian mourning wear — constrictive, torn in places, spotted with black mould that matches her hair — and never without her sunglasses or a long black parasol that always looks like it is dripping. She carries herself like nobility because she was bred to, and talks like a pretentious high elf on purpose, to people who least expect a rebel to sound that way. She is the party's healer, and heals the way other people pick fights: it is her rebellion. Fragile as glass up close, so she stays at range and sends mushrooms in. Prays to someone she calls the Red Lady, whom she reaches through fungus and long conversation. Her name means "bad trip," and she is in on the joke.

**Now:** Health **Normal** · Tokens **12** · Gold **20** (+20 in the group fund) · as of Session Two (2026-09-09). Log: [Iter Bravus](../Character%20Logs/Iter%20Bravus.md).

## ABILITIES

See [Abilities](../Mechanics/Abilities.md) for full entries. Casting rules: [Casting](../Mechanics/Questing%20Time!%20Mechanics.md#casting).

**[Gifted Channel](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#gifted-channel)** — her casting access. Roll Charisma to cast; targets resist with Instinct. A Gifted caster doesn't analyze a spell, she asks her patron for it: "Red Lady, give me strength."

**[Night Vision](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#night-vision)** — racial. Sees perfectly in the dark. In bright light, before any check that depends on seeing, she rolls Constitution first; on a failure the check is at disadvantage. Her sunglasses cancel the penalty.

**[Healing Spore Cloud](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#healing-spore-cloud)** — cantrip. Gifted Restoration. A burst of spores heals every friendly target in the radius by one step on the Health track. Ranged. Action only, once per turn.

**[Fungal Shield](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#fungal-shield)** — Gifted Abjuration. A large mushroom springs up and takes the blow, reducing incoming damage by one step. Blocks physical attacks and Evocation only. Reaction or action, up to twice a round.

## PATRON

**The Red Lady.** Iter's patron, and the source of her Gifted magic. "The Red Lady" is a name Iter invented — the entity has never given its own, and Iter has never asked, because why would she care. She reaches her through mushrooms and long stretches of prayer and conversation. At Session Zero the Red Lady told her only to *find* the man coming out of the caves, never to follow him: "It's about a place, darling. It's about a place."
