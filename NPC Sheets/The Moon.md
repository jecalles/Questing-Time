The Moon: a Majestic Celestial, "her". Since Session Five [Bob](../Player%20Sheets/Baeon%20"Bob"%20Oberoni.md) casts through her: he asks, nicely, and she answers. See [Pantheon](../Lore/Magic%20System.md#pantheon).

## WHAT THE PARTY KNOWS

- Troubled when Bob was weighed and found heavy, disappointed the second time. After the third: "Darling, he'll get it one day."
- Told Bob his copy of [Snarfa](../NPC%20Sheets/Snarfa.md)'s privacy dome was a wonderful spell, and to ask more.
- The [Red Lady](../NPC%20Sheets/Red%20Lady.md) says she has been wrapped up for a few years with one human man, and is waning more than waxing.
- [Ra](../NPC%20Sheets/Ra.md) says he and the Moon used to date.
