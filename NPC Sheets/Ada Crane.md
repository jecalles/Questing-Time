**Human · she/her · Inspector, City Council finance department · Castle investigator**

Birdlike and curious; long, scrawny and wry. Five foot eleven, with a real moustache coiled at both ends and a monocle. Out of disguise she dresses like a gentleman of another age: bowler hat, waistcoat, pocket watch. Earnest, well meaning and bumbling, now and then brilliant, and lucky more often than that. She works for [the Castle](../Lore/Places/The%20Castle.md), not [the College](../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md). She is bad at disguises and worse at names: at [Snarfa](../NPC%20Sheets/Snarfa.md)'s party she was a student called Studentsson, in a Hawaiian shirt and a fake moustache over her real one, and stuck out like a sore thumb. She walks fast, hips going, elbows bent.

**Now:** With the party in [the schoolhouse library](../Lore/Places/The%20Schoolhouse%20Library.md) across the river, limping from the beam, before dawn · as of Session Six (2026-10-07).

## WHAT THE PARTY KNOWS

- She came to [Snarfa](../NPC%20Sheets/Snarfa.md)'s party as "Studentsson", from "a long line of students", in a Hawaiian shirt she called a family heirloom. She does not drink: "I'm too studious."
- She walked out of [Alistrassa](../Player%20Sheets/Alistrassa.md)'s salt circle untouched. Alistrassa read her as deceitful, but not malicious.
- Under the fake moustache is a real one. "My name is Ada Crane": an inspector of the City Council's finance department, and "the whole team. This is not a well funded department."
- "Something about the books aren't adding up." She has audited most of the Bureaucratic Council. The Academic Council has been "very not forthcoming", more from disorganisation than malice.
- "Who's buying these bat wings at 400 gold bars a piece?" Her books show the 400 bars on the whole line: two wings.
- [The College](../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md)'s numbers added up for years, so the Council told her to look away. Now they change from one day to the next, "just so subtly", and the trail points at [Transmutation](../Lore/Places/Transmutation.md).
- She is coming back with her books, to talk upstairs under a privacy dome.
- [Ember](../Player%20Sheets/Ember%20Spanner.md) blew her road and took her ledgers; the party got them back.
- Two bat wings at 400 gold bars, "the entire Western Garrison for about eight months". The next day her own notes read 4 gold 18 silver, and the working beside them had changed too.
- Her supervisor napped all day with access to her desk, and shrugged.

## ALIGNMENT

Toward [Ember](../Player%20Sheets/Ember%20Spanner.md): Neutral. Toward the party: Neutral.

## LAST BEAT

Session Six: buried under a beam when [Ember](../Player%20Sheets/Ember%20Spanner.md) blew the road; dug out by [Pudge](../Player%20Sheets/Pudge.md). Went with the party to [the schoolhouse library](../Lore/Places/The%20Schoolhouse%20Library.md) and told them her story over her own books.
