**Human · she/her · Inspector, City Council finance department · Castle investigator**

Birdlike and curious; long, scrawny and wry. Five foot eleven, with a real moustache coiled at both ends and a monocle. Out of disguise she dresses like a gentleman of another age: bowler hat, waistcoat, pocket watch. Earnest, well meaning and bumbling, now and then brilliant, and lucky more often than that. She works for the Castle, not the College. She is bad at disguises and worse at names: at Snarfa's party she was a student called Studentsson, in a Hawaiian shirt and a fake moustache over her real one, and stuck out like a sore thumb. She walks fast, hips going, elbows bent.

**Now:** Gone for her books; meeting the party upstairs in Abjuration within the hour · as of Session Five (2026-09-30).

## WHAT THE PARTY KNOWS

- She came to Snarfa's party as "Studentsson", from "a long line of students", in a Hawaiian shirt she called a family heirloom. She does not drink: "I'm too studious."
- She walked out of Alistrassa's salt circle untouched. Alistrassa read her as deceitful, but not malicious.
- Under the fake moustache is a real one. "My name is Ada Crane": an inspector of the City Council's finance department, and "the whole team. This is not a well funded department."
- "Something about the books aren't adding up." She has audited most of the Bureaucratic Council. The Academic Council has been "very not forthcoming", more from disorganisation than malice.
- "Who's buying these bat wings at 400 gold bars a piece?"
- The College's numbers added up for years, so the Council told her to look away. Now they change from one day to the next, "just so subtly", and the trail points at Transmutation.
- She is coming back with her books, to talk upstairs under a privacy dome.
