The Red Lady: [Iter](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md)'s patron. See [Pantheon](../Lore/Magic%20System.md#pantheon).

## WHAT THE PARTY KNOWS

A slate-grey meadow, one scarlet figure: short dress, wide pointed hat, a silver bauble. She meets [Iter](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md) in mushroom trances.

At Session Seven she was low and worried about the Moon, who she says has been wrapped up for a few years with one human man and is waning more than waxing. She gave Iter a glowing head and let her upgrade her spore cloud to hurt enemies too. She does not know the sand, and does not know the scales figure: "Stay away from him." She sighs at Yuhai's name. She asked Iter to keep an eye on it and to pry.
