**Munchkin · he/him · Head of the Zuccaro family, [the Red Line](../Lore/Places/The%20Red%20Line.md)'s old blood**

He wears a white suit with a black collar and a bow tie, smokes a sugar-plum cigar, keeps a cat, and pours cognac older than most of his guests. His house is old stone on the district's oldest street, with red leather and a library of old leather. He has not laid a hand on anyone in years; that is what [Ziggy](../NPC%20Sheets/Ziggy.md) and [Slo Mo](../NPC%20Sheets/Slo%20Mo.md) are for. He answers questions honestly, and it tells you nothing. He calls the party friends of the family.

**Now:** In his parlour, waiting on [Finnegan](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md) · Feast Week · as of Session Four (2026-09-24).

## WHAT THE PARTY KNOWS

- He hired the party to bring [Finnegan](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md) home, paid 50 gold on delivery and 50 more for the next job, then hired them to get Finnegan back.
- Under [Lark](../Player%20Sheets/Lark%20Myca%20Roux.md)'s serum ("allergies"): his people predate the city, they kill for [the Red Line](../Lore/Places/The%20Red%20Line.md) for pay, messages go back and forth with [the College](../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md), and he has never touched Finnegan or set foot in the College.
- He blamed the pirates and Bramblebeard for the raid, and lent the party [Tommy Two Cheeks](../NPC%20Sheets/Tommy%20Two%20Cheeks.md).
- He sent Bob to [Munge](../NPC%20Sheets/Munge.md) for work, and hinted at College construction work at city hall.
- [Ziggy](../NPC%20Sheets/Ziggy.md) is his son. [Nonna](../NPC%20Sheets/Nonna.md), his great-aunt, cooks.
- The party sleeps in a Zuccaro safe house.
- The party's read: he did not order [Hamlet](../Lore/Places/Hamlet.md), or did not know.
- Munge carried him the party's message: they will bring Finnegan once healed.
