**Munchkin · he/him · Head of the Zuccaro family, the Red Line's old blood**

He wears a white suit with a black collar and a bow tie, smokes a sugar-plum cigar, keeps a cat, and pours cognac older than most of his guests. His house is old stone on the district's oldest street, with red leather and a library of old leather. He has not laid a hand on anyone in years; that is what Ziggy and Slo Mo are for. He answers questions honestly, and it tells you nothing. He calls the party friends of the family.

**Now:** In his parlour, waiting on Finnegan · Feast Week · as of Session Four (2026-09-24).

## WHAT THE PARTY KNOWS

- He hired the party to bring Finnegan home, paid 50 gold on delivery and 50 more for the next job, then hired them to get Finnegan back.
- Under Lark's serum ("allergies"): his people predate the city, they kill for the Red Line for pay, messages go back and forth with the College, and he has never touched Finnegan or set foot in the College.
- He blamed the pirates and Bramblebeard for the raid, and lent the party Tommy Two Cheeks.
- He sent Bob to Munge for work, and hinted at College construction work at city hall.
- Ziggy is his son. Nonna, his great-aunt, cooks.
- The party sleeps in a Zuccaro safe house.
- The party's read: he did not order Hamlet, or did not know.
- Munge carried him the party's message: they will bring Finnegan once healed.
