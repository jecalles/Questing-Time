**Half-giant · he/him · the Zuccaro family's muscle**

Eight feet tall and long-armed, in a faded black leather vest with the sleeves torn off and a small red bow tie. He came to the Zuccaros as a lost boy seven years ago and has been the family's muscle since. He is soft-spoken and careful, a big man used to being gentle. He was beaten nearly to death guarding [Finnegan](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md), and he shot himself with a blunderbuss at [the depot](../Lore/Places/The%20Red%20Line%20Depot.md). He is somewhat smitten with [Iter](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md).

**Now:** Minor damage · gone to [the Don](../NPC%20Sheets/Don%20Aldo%20Zuccaro.md) with the party's message · as of Session Four (2026-09-24).

## WHAT THE PARTY KNOWS

- "Seven years." He came to the family as a lost boy.
- He was found behind [the Don](../NPC%20Sheets/Don%20Aldo%20Zuccaro.md)'s back door, arm broken and eyes swollen shut: "They got him."
- [Iter](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md) healed him; he came to [the depot](../Lore/Places/The%20Red%20Line%20Depot.md) with a club, and he and [Pudge](../Player%20Sheets/Pudge.md) built the crates into a staircase.
- At the depot he picked up a looted blunderbuss, which he has never learned to use, and shot himself in the chest.
- Bob's job offer from the Don came through him.
- He left at the start of Session Four with the party's message, and believed every word of it.
