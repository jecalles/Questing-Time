**Half-giant · he/him · the Zuccaro family's muscle**

Eight feet tall and long-armed, in a faded black leather vest with the sleeves torn off and a small red bow tie. He came to the Zuccaros as a lost boy seven years ago and has been the family's muscle since. He is soft-spoken and careful, a big man used to being gentle. He was beaten nearly to death guarding Finnegan, and he shot himself with a blunderbuss at the depot. He is somewhat smitten with Iter.

**Now:** Minor damage · gone to the Don with the party's message · as of Session Four (2026-09-24).
