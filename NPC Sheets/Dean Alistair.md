**High Elf · he/him · Dean of Restoration, College of Mages**

Old, in a long white robe with a long white beard and the hat. His given name is Dean, so he is "Dean Dean," to the table's lasting amusement. He reads his books up the Restoration spire, behind a fairy receptionist, and is not doing much else. He is blunt about death and sorry about it. He funds a karmic-restoration desk nobody would sit at, and he hired Alistrassa to it the day she arrived. A read on him says he seems kind, and nothing more.

**Now:** Up the Restoration spire · as of Session Four (2026-09-24).

## WHAT THE PARTY KNOWS

- He hired Alistrassa to the karmic-restoration desk, where the last holder died at his work.
- He told her: "There's a good chance that no matter what I do, you are certainly going to die."
- He sent her after the party, on a rumour of adventurers carrying a College rune. Alistrassa thinks she is disposable to him.
- He chairs the Academic Council, and that makes him the face New Squishnesta City Council sees.
