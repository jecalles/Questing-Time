Lark's squirrels: Larry, Curly, The Bald One I Think, Yes and No. They ride around [Lark](../Player%20Sheets/Lark%20Myca%20Roux.md)'s neck, a chain of them under her cloak. See [Friend of the Forest](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#friend-of-the-forest).

## WHAT THE PARTY KNOWS

- One slept in [Snarfa](../NPC%20Sheets/Snarfa.md)'s empty wine goblet through the whole of Session Six, fell out, took the fall badly, and climbed back onto her neck.
- The Bald One I Think had a sticker for [Ember](../Player%20Sheets/Ember%20Spanner.md), from [Lark](../Player%20Sheets/Lark%20Myca%20Roux.md)'s travels. Lark launched him from a catapult built of a gas tank. He landed two roofs away, dazed, still holding it. Later he put it on Ember's forehead and gave a thumbs up.
- At Session Seven Lark gave The Bald One I Think to [Pudge](../Player%20Sheets/Pudge.md).
