**The super senior · he/him · the [Abjuration](../Lore/Places/Abjuration.md) clerk at the College of Mages**

Fifty years a student at [The College of Mages](../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md), never graduated, better than some of the professors and paid to teach. He sits the Abjuration clerk's desk in flip-flops, a fleece, board shorts and a backwards cap, frequently high, and gets about on a longboard. He looks like a dog and is explicitly not a dog: golden, with big floppy ears. Nobody has explained this and nobody asks. Smoke rings off his joint hang in the air as runes, the numbers in his sudoku get up and dance, and his magic reads as more flowing than other wizards'. He knows [the sun god](../NPC%20Sheets/Ra.md) personally and carries a phone nobody can explain.

**Now:** Leg broken, in a cast at the Abjuration desk · out of spell slots · as of Session Six (2026-10-07).

## WHAT THE PARTY KNOWS

- He answered [Alistrassa](../Player%20Sheets/Alistrassa.md)'s chit at once, came to [the depot](../Lore/Places/The%20Red%20Line%20Depot.md) on a longboard, and has helped ever since. He will not always be around.
- He learned glassblowing from [Ellis](../NPC%20Sheets/Ellis.md) once.
- He can call [Ra](../NPC%20Sheets/Ra.md). Ra once melted a thirty-foot cone into the [Abjuration](../Lore/Places/Abjuration.md) floor.
- He put [Iter](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md) back together after she was decapitated at the depot, with his last two spell slots.
- He dropped [Bartholomew](../NPC%20Sheets/Bartholomew%20Groundshaker.md) into another plane during the heist. Bartholomew is not back.
- He drew the killing rune out of the velvet pouch before it reached Alistrassa.
- He put up a privacy shield for Alistrassa and agreed to keep what she said inside it.
- He gave [Finnegan](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md) a new face, then silenced him by accident.
- He has heard of a wizard raising a man from a fingernail. He costs Finnegan's raising from an arm at about a week, with blood diamonds and goblin fangs.
- He threw the party in [the quad](../Lore/Places/The%20Quad.md), fronting his band, the Snarfettes. [Pudge](../Player%20Sheets/Pudge.md) thinks he sings a little too well.
- [Calliope](../NPC%20Sheets/Calliope.md) is an old friend: "She was once my muse."
- He would not stop Ra's beam himself: "He's really strong."
- He kissed Iter at the end of the night and left with her.
