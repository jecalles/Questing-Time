**The super senior · he/him · the Abjuration clerk at the College of Mages**

Fifty years a student at [The College of Mages](../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md), never graduated, better than some of the professors and paid to teach. He sits the Abjuration clerk's desk in flip-flops, a fleece, board shorts and a backwards cap, frequently high, and gets about on a longboard. He looks like a dog and is explicitly not a dog: golden, with big floppy ears. Nobody has explained this and nobody asks. Smoke rings off his joint hang in the air as runes, the numbers in his sudoku get up and dance, and his magic reads as more flowing than other wizards'. He knows the sun god personally and carries a phone nobody can explain.

**Now:** Unhurt · out of spell slots · the party in the quad winding down; gone off with Iter · as of Session Five (2026-09-30).

## WHAT THE PARTY KNOWS

- He answered Alistrassa's chit at once, came to the depot on a longboard, and has helped ever since. He will not always be around.
- He learned glassblowing from Ellis once.
- He can call Ra. Ra once melted a thirty-foot cone into the Abjuration floor.
- He put Iter back together after she was decapitated at the depot, with his last two spell slots.
- He dropped Bartholomew into another plane during the heist. Bartholomew is not back.
- He drew the killing rune out of the velvet pouch before it reached Alistrassa.
- He put up a privacy shield for Alistrassa and agreed to keep what she said inside it.
- He gave Finnegan a new face, then silenced him by accident.
- He has heard of a wizard raising a man from a fingernail. He costs Finnegan's raising from an arm at about a week, with blood diamonds and goblin fangs.
- He threw the party in the quad, fronting his band, the Snarfettes. Pudge thinks he sings a little too well.
- Calliope is an old friend: "She was once my muse."
- He would not stop Ra's beam himself: "He's really strong."
- He kissed Iter at the end of the night and left with her.
