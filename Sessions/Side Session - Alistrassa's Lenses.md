# Side Session — Alistrassa's Lenses

*Played one-on-one on 2026-09-06, while the rest of the party was in the
Red Line. This is where [Alistrassa](../Player%20Sheets/Alistrassa.md) has been,
and it is why she walks into Session Two already knowing the building.*

Alistrassa is a high elf wizard with a karmic wasting disease. She has
about five years, and at the end of them there is no reincarnation
waiting; the chain simply stops. Casting from instinct rather than from
study makes it worse, so every shortcut she takes costs her something
she cannot get back.

She arrived at [the College of Mages](../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md) on her
first day and went straight for a dean, which is not how it is done.

The College is a hexagon: six schools, a spire each, small rooms, a
shared quad, thirty-odd academics, books on every shelf and an IOU
economy of chits to get at them. She met [Tim](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#tim-aboul)
at the Restoration clinic, who explained that humans practise none of
it. She met [Martha](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#martha-woolsworth) in procurement. She
got past a blunt fairy receptionist to [Dean
Alistair](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#dean-alistair) of Restoration, an old elf in white, who had a funded post
that nobody wanted: karmic restoration. As he put it, the only people
who care are the people who are currently dying. The last holder of the
post had died at his desk of the same disease Alistrassa has. She took
the job, and inherited his messy library and his caseload, all of them
deceased.

A two-week research project ran to four months. She wanted a lens that
would carry a spell so she would not have to cast it. To get one she
assembled most of a faculty: [Snarfa](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#snarfa), fifty years
a super senior, flip-flops and fleece, better than some of the
professors and paid to teach; [Penny](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#penny), nineteen, on
her second coffee, who could not get the calligraphic flick and then
could; [Ellis](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#ellis), a Munchkin glassmaker from the south
end of town who grinds bifocals; [Jeff](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#jeff) of Evocation,
whose answer to most problems is more fire; and [Eustace](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#eustace)
and [Muriel](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#muriel) of Transmutation, arguing about
conservation laws over a transmittative ink.

The rig projects a rune through inked paper into glass, and it needs a
light that nobody at the College can supply. So Snarfa phoned the sun
god. [Ra](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#ra) came through a portal eagle-faced and golden,
made his heartbeat the bass line of a party in the Abjuration hall, and
etched fifty-one lenses in a row without a miss. It was an assembly
line, and it worked.

One lens quietly runs her identify spell, for free, forever. The other
fifty are a set of primitives that between them span the common runes,
so that common incantations can be composed from them the way words are
set from a case of type.

She walks into Session Two wearing it: glasses with slide-in lenses, a
blueprint for a reprogrammable rune projector, a notebook of people who
owe her a favour, a post in Restoration, and a folio. She is also losing
track of time, and the disease is why. See
[Alistrassa's Lenses](../Mechanics/Items%20and%20Equipment.md#alistrassas-lenses).

## What your characters know

- Alistrassa has been inside the College for four months. She knows the
  building, its six schools, and a dozen of its people by name.
- Her clock is real and it is short. She is looking for a cure, and she
  has reason to think the College is where it is.
- Martha, the Munchkin clerk whose sealed envelopes Finnegan was
  carrying to Bill the Butcher, works a few doors from people Alistrassa
  now knows by name.
- Anything at a common casting level she can compose from the set she
  carries. Her work now is discovery: new inscriptions, new geometries,
  and, for anything greater, new glass.
