# Session One — the Red Line and the Don

*Played 2026-09-02. Previously: Session Zero, the envelope run.*

The session opened where Session Zero stopped, outside the [Hamlet](../Lore/Places/Hamlet.md)
tavern. [Finnegan](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md) admitted the deed he was promised was his only pay
and his money was "in a different place." [Iter](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md) sent him to fetch a
flower so the party could talk. He never came back. Tracks in the
grass said four small sets went out and three came back, heading for
the city.

The party ran them down in a quarter hour: three Munchkins in
pinstripe suits, [Tommy](../NPC%20Sheets/Tommy%20Two%20Cheeks.md) guns, Finnegan over a shoulder. [Ziggy](../NPC%20Sheets/Ziggy.md) did the
talking, [Slo Mo](../NPC%20Sheets/Slo%20Mo.md) did not hurry, Tommy Two Cheeks kept quiet. They
were taking him to [the Don](../NPC%20Sheets/Don%20Aldo%20Zuccaro.md) to be killed. [Lark](../Player%20Sheets/Lark%20Myca%20Roux.md) proposed a deal: the
party helps deliver him, gets a cut, and gets its own fee out of him
first. Three safeties clicked off when Bob suggested the alternative.
Deal struck. The party named the trio "Terry."

They camped outside the city. Bob sat under the moon with a glowing
toadstool on his chest and reached for something; what answered was
not what he reached for, and it left him rested and embarrassed. Lark
found her old recipe, foraged frog sweat, a sprig of yew, and cinnamon
out of Slo Mo's tea bag, and brewed a truth serum so far past her
usual work that it came out Greater. A quarter dose on Finnegan: he
has never had money; Bill the Butcher owes him a gold and change in
wages; Bill is the neighbourhood's grocer and the man everything
passes through; Bramblebeard is a pirate who trusts only Bill; [Martha](../NPC%20Sheets/Martha%20Woolsworth.md)
is a Munchkin clerk at [the College](../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md) who lives in [the Red Line](../Lore/Places/The%20Red%20Line.md); and
Finnegan carried sealed envelopes from Martha to Bill without reading
them. Why does everyone want him dead? He had never thought about it.
He knows what everyone is doing, when, where, and for how much. Tommy
asked about his sister. Finnegan said yes. Tommy hugged him and called
him family.

In the morning, into the Red Line: a band of red brick around a
district of boarded houses, sweet fog, and Munchkins mainlining candy.
Don Zuccaro received them in a white suit with a cat and a sugar-plum
cigar, and paid 50 gold on the spot for bringing his associate home.
Iter danced a drow courtesy dance, Bob rang a cowbell, and Lark's
"allergies" put a vaporised dose of serum in the Don's face. Under it
he answered every question and gave away nothing that hurt the family:
his people predate the city, they kill for the Red Line for pay,
messages go back and forth with the College, and he personally has
never touched Finnegan and never set foot in the College. Bob stood up
to his full height and asked for work; the Don sent him to [Munge](../NPC%20Sheets/Munge.md), his
eight-foot half-giant. The party became friends of the family.

Then a wet thud at a back door. Munge lay behind it, arm broken, eyes
swollen shut: "They got him." Finnegan was gone, taken by Munchkins
with guns and hammers. Iter healed Munge from critical to merely
broken. The Don blamed the pirates and Bramblebeard, sent Tommy Two
Cheeks along with the party, and paid another 50 gold. [Nonna](../NPC%20Sheets/Nonna.md) cooked
candy spaghetti. Iter's stomach objected. The party slept in a Zuccaro
safe house with 20 gold each, 20 in the group fund, a live death rune
in Lark's pack, and a Munchkin to find for the second time.

## What your characters know

- [Finnegan](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md) is alive, majorly hurt, and was taken from [the Don](../NPC%20Sheets/Don%20Aldo%20Zuccaro.md)'s back
 room by armed Munchkins. The Don thinks it was the pirates.
- Bill the Butcher is the neighbourhood's food and distribution hub,
 owes Finnegan wages, and is where the Don says to go for provisions.
- Bramblebeard is a pirate "by the docks," moving sweet product
 inland, and trusts only Bill.
- [Martha](../NPC%20Sheets/Martha%20Woolsworth.md) is a Munchkin procurement clerk at [the College of Mages](../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md) who
 commutes into [the Red Line](../Lore/Places/The%20Red%20Line.md). Finnegan carried her sealed envelopes to
 Bill.
- The Don's family predates the city, does killing for pay, and
 exchanges messages with the College through go-betweens. He has
 never been inside the College.
- [New Squishnesta](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md) has been under austerity for two or three years to
 pay for a war. The Munchkins do not consider it theirs.
- The death rune was written in plain black ink. [Pudge](../Player%20Sheets/Pudge.md) noticed. He
 does not know why it matters.
- Bob has not checked in with his College handler.
- [Tommy Two Cheeks](../NPC%20Sheets/Tommy%20Two%20Cheeks.md) travels with you and reports to the Don. He has a
 dent in his head from a brick and is very fond of you.
