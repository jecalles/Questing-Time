# Quest Log

The jobs you've taken on, and where each one stands. Current to Session
Seven; rewritten after every session.

## Open

**Finnegan.** You got Finnegan back from the depot for the Don at Session
Three, and you never delivered him. You told the Don, through Munge, that
you'd bring him once you'd healed and learned more. Your own plan was to
take his arm, let the Don have his death, and have Snarfa raise him
somewhere else under a new face. The face and the name, Seamus, were done.
At Session Seven he was gone from the library in the night. The moth saw
"enormous munchkins" and violence. You are not chasing him, and you can pick
the thread up if you like.

**Who wanted Finnegan dead?** Someone sent him a death rune in an envelope,
by way of Hamlet. Your read: not the Don, or not with the Don's knowledge,
and perhaps the College, over the laundering. Pudge recalls the rune as a
hurried professional job, not in the draftsman's own ink.

**Ada's books.** Ember took most of Ada's papers and left in the night.
Alistrassa's lens showed the edits glowing pale beige. At Session Seven
the glowing sand lifted the ink off the ledgers and showed the true
entries, the same line again and again: "Bardo material, one crate, two
gold bars". Ada reads the books as cooking themselves.

**A Bob on the list.** Alistrassa's wasting list has sixteen names crossed
out and a seventeenth circled, and it says Bob. Everyone else on it is dead.

**The College's construction.** The Don says the College is building
something and wants regulation pushed through city hall, and he offered you
the work. Nobody has said what it is.

## Personal

**The envelope — Pudge.** Thornbush gave the order again: get the envelope
to Finnegan, confirm he has it, report back. Finnegan is gone and the rune
is still in your pack.

**Runes for a dying wizard — Alistrassa.** A cure for her wasting disease,
or a way to cast without paying for it. Both roads lead to whoever studies
karma. In the meantime: the ROD, and a light source for it.

## Done

**Deliver Finnegan to the Don.** Done at Session One: 100 gold, 50 on
delivery and 50 more for the next job.

**Recover Finnegan.** Done at Session Three. Where he went next is under
Open.
