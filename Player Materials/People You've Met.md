# People You've Met

Who you've met, where, and what you know. Only what the table has heard.
Someone with a sheet of their own links to it.

## Around the party

### Ember

A teenager with a long brown braid, in baggy forest-green fatigues. Blew the road and took [Ada](../NPC%20Sheets/Ada%20Crane.md)'s books, then led you over the river. A shadow follows her. Took you to a library she trusts. Sheet: [Ember Spanner](../Player%20Sheets/Ember%20Spanner.md).

### Seamus

Half Munchkin, half human. Finnegan Bramblebush, until Session Four: now a glamoured face, blonde and flat-nosed, and no voice at all. Wanted dead by more people than anyone can count. Tied to the couch in [Abjuration](../Lore/Places/Abjuration.md). Brightens the moment anyone is kind to him.

Sheet: [Seamus](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md).

### Munge

Half-giant, eight feet, [the Don](../NPC%20Sheets/Don%20Aldo%20Zuccaro.md)'s muscle. A faded black leather vest with the sleeves torn off, a small red bow tie. Came to the Zuccaros as a lost boy. Beaten nearly to death guarding Finnegan; shot himself with a blunderbuss at [the depot](../Lore/Places/The%20Red%20Line%20Depot.md). Soft-spoken. Gone to the Don with your message.

Sheet: [Munge](../NPC%20Sheets/Munge.md).

### Tommy Two Cheeks

The Don's man, with you since Session One. Hat sideways, a dent in his head from a brick, cheeks packed with candy. Reports back to the Don, and you know it.

Sheet: [Tommy Two Cheeks](../NPC%20Sheets/Tommy%20Two%20Cheeks.md).

## The Red Line

### Don Aldo Zuccaro

Head of the Zuccaro family, [the Red Line](../Lore/Places/The%20Red%20Line.md)'s old blood. White suit, black collar, bow tie, a sugar-plum cigar, a cat, cognac older than most of his guests. Hired you to deliver [Finnegan](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md), then to get him back. Calls you friends of the family.

Sheet: [Don Aldo Zuccaro](../NPC%20Sheets/Don%20Aldo%20Zuccaro.md).

### Ziggy

The Don's son. His runner and talker. Pinstripe suit, Tommy gun. Moves before the Don finishes a sentence.

Sheet: [Ziggy](../NPC%20Sheets/Ziggy.md).

### Slo Mo

The Don's heavy. Never hurries, never speaks first, does not move when told to. Tea, a spice bag, candy bars.

Sheet: [Slo Mo](../NPC%20Sheets/Slo%20Mo.md).

### Nonna

The Don's great-aunt, in the kitchen. Candy spaghetti, candied-grape syrup, peppermint pasta.

Sheet: [Nonna](../NPC%20Sheets/Nonna.md).

### Robert

Runs [the Tavern](../Lore/Places/The%20Tavern.md). Bob knew him on sight for a fellow follower of Yuhai.

Sheet: [Robert](../NPC%20Sheets/Robert.md).

### Ellis

A Munchkin spectacles shop by the Red Line, everything Munchkin-sized. Grinds lenses in a few weeks. Taught [Snarfa](../NPC%20Sheets/Snarfa.md) glassblowing. "I'm no magician."

Sheet: [Ellis](../NPC%20Sheets/Ellis.md).

## The depot

### Bartholomew Groundshaker

Dwarf, [the depot](../Lore/Places/The%20Red%20Line%20Depot.md)'s engineer, an Australian accent. Happy to show a stranger how the train sigils repel. [Iter](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md) danced him into a stupor; [Snarfa](../NPC%20Sheets/Snarfa.md) dropped him into another plane. Not back yet.

Sheet: [Bartholomew Groundshaker](../NPC%20Sheets/Bartholomew%20Groundshaker.md).

## The College

### Snarfa

The super senior: fifty years a student, never graduated, better than some of the professors and paid to teach. The [Abjuration](../Lore/Places/Abjuration.md) clerk's desk, flip-flops, a fleece, board shorts. Looks like a dog; is not a dog. Knows the sun god personally. Threw the party in [the quad](../Lore/Places/The%20Quad.md), fronting his band, the Snarfettes.

Broke his leg after his party; in a cast at his desk.

Sheet: [Snarfa](../NPC%20Sheets/Snarfa.md).

### Dean Alistair

Dean of [Restoration](../Lore/Places/Restoration.md). Old, a long white robe, a long white beard, the hat. His given name is Dean. Hired [Alistrassa](../Player%20Sheets/Alistrassa.md) to the karmic-restoration desk the day she arrived, and sent her after you.

Sheet: [Dean Alistair](../NPC%20Sheets/Dean%20Alistair.md).

### Martha Woolsworth

[Procurement](../Lore/Places/Procurement.md). A Munchkin, one of the few Munchkin wizards. Brisk and kind, likes her ledger tidy; her manifests are perfect. Looking into the sand for Alistrassa.

Sheet: [Martha Woolsworth](../NPC%20Sheets/Martha%20Woolsworth.md).

### Mark Thornbush

[Pudge](../Player%20Sheets/Pudge.md)'s handler. A small, nervous wizard. Briefed Pudge in a College basement: shadow the incoming party, make sure the quest succeeds, receive an envelope. Wrote by insect familiar to ask whether Pudge has [Finnegan](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md) and the envelope.

At Session Five he gave Pudge the same order again, four times: get the envelope to Finnegan, and send word. A Saturday off and a ticket to Seaside if he does. He calls himself an underdean.

Sheet: [Mark Thornbush](../NPC%20Sheets/Mark%20Thornbush.md).

### Penny

First-year, Abjuration. Nineteen, bangs, her second coffee. Likes runes because they are pretty. Warded [Iter](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md)'s parasol for two books. Has Iter's parasol and new canvas, and a book on runic projection mapping. Session Six: finished the runes for the parasol and the cloak. Distant and tired; midterms.

Sheet: [Penny](../NPC%20Sheets/Penny.md).

### Jeff

[Evocation](../Lore/Places/Evocation.md). A fire sorcerer in athletic gear; wisps come off him when he talks. Had never heard of a blunderbuss.

Sheet: [Jeff](../NPC%20Sheets/Jeff.md).

### Tim Aboul

The Restoration clinic. Bald, white moustache, monocle. Does not treat karma: "We practise none of it."

Sheet: [Tim Aboul](../NPC%20Sheets/Tim%20Aboul.md).

### Muriel

[Transmutation](../Lore/Places/Transmutation.md). An elf, Eustace's bench partner and corrector. Made the ink that let light through paper.

Sheet: [Muriel](../NPC%20Sheets/Muriel.md).

### Eustace

Transmutation. A gnome, a transmuter who builds things. Grabs cheeks.

Sheet: [Eustace](../NPC%20Sheets/Eustace.md).

### Ra

The sun god. Eagle-headed, golden, poolside. Snarfa's friend. Once melted a thirty-foot cone into the Abjuration floor; etched Alistrassa's lenses at a party. Came to Snarfa's party, fired early through Alistrassa's lenses, and killed Bob. Says he and the Moon used to date.

Sheet: [Ra](../NPC%20Sheets/Ra.md).

### Calliope

Eldest of the Muses, and an old friend of Snarfa's: "She was once my muse." Came to his party in a column of white mist, long grey hair, eyes that change colour as you look. Sang until Ra jumped her closing number.

Sheet: [Calliope](../NPC%20Sheets/Calliope.md).

### Ada Crane

An inspector of the City Council's finance department, and "the whole team". Came to Snarfa's party as Studentsson, a student in a Hawaiian shirt and a fake moustache; under it, a real one. Asking who buys bat wings at 400 gold bars each. Coming back with her books.

In the library she told you her story: two bat wings at 400 gold bars, and figures that change after she writes them.

Sheet: [Ada Crane](../NPC%20Sheets/Ada%20Crane.md).

### Fillibus

A College professor who drank a rainbow potion: his pupils went rectangular, with rainbows across them, and he ran naked round the party. Talked Ra's beam down.

Sheet: [Fillibus](../NPC%20Sheets/Fillibus.md).

### Jimmy

About twelve, in black face paint, on a guitar with no holes and no cable. Guitarist in Snarfa's band, the Snarfettes, with a goblin and a drow. Snarfa picked him up in a small town. A prodigy.

Sheet: [Jimmy](../NPC%20Sheets/Jimmy.md).
