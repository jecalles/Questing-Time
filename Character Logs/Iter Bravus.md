## Session Zero (2026-08-29)

- Emerged from the caves under [New Squishnesta](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md) alongside [Bob](../Player%20Sheets/Baeon%20"Bob"%20Oberoni.md), having led blind Bob through the dark for days, following the Red Lady's vision — "find a man stumbling out of the depths of a cave."
- The Red Lady's message, clarified in play: she only ever said *find* the man, never *follow* him, and never spoke of a "path." Her actual guidance: "**It's about a place, darling. It's about a place.**"
- Became **aligned with the whole party** on a bonding roll. Her bonding vignette: got very high on cave mushrooms, connected with the Red Lady.
- The Red Lady / menstruation argument with Lark: Iter kept talking "to learn more," took disadvantage for the rest of that conversation, stayed aligned with Lark.
- Took the "tripping" condition from Lark's hotbox (elected to fail the save).
- Weakening as the moon wanes — the spore-druid phase mechanic surfaced.
- Parasol ruled a one-handed piercing melee weapon.
- In the Finnegan fight: raised a tree-sized mushroom through the tavern floor under him (charisma 15 vs 8) — knocked him prone, one damage step; wrecked the floor.

## Session One (2026-09-02)

Sent Finnegan off to pick a flower so the party could talk, and lost him to three Munchkin gangsters. Linked arms with the Don's men on the road and drew their life stories out of them; took tea with Ziggy. Danced a drow "courtesy dance" to distract Don Zuccaro while Lark dosed him, and it worked. Healed Munge, the Don's half-giant, from critical to major after the raid. Ate Nonna's candy spaghetti and regretted it (Constitution 1). Now a friend of the Zuccaro family.

**State at close:** Health Normal · Tokens 10 · Gold 20 (+20 in the group fund).

## Session Two (2026-09-09)

- Healed Munge twice more with the spore cloud — once at the safe house, once on the road to the depot — until he was nearly whole and could carry a club. Let Alistrassa step into the cloud too; it eased her physical exhaustion (not her disease).
- Ran the distraction lock on **Bartholomew Groundshaker** at the depot gate: danced, flirted, offered water and mints. Her Constitution exploded (4+3), so he stayed charmed and oblivious for a very long time while the others raided the warehouse.
- On the plan to walk into the pirates and lie: rolled instinct 12 and read it plainly — "you're going to get absolutely fucking murdered; they'd just see you as an obstacle." The party hid instead.

**State at close:** Health Normal · Tokens 12 · Gold 20 (+20 in the group fund).

## Session Three (2026-09-16)

- Raised an eight-foot mushroom through the undercarriage of the departing train and **turned it over**, stopping Finnegan leaving the depot.
- Scooped Finnegan out of the wreck with the parasol, and scooped him back again when he slipped loose.
- **Held her reaction** rather than spending it — the first time anyone at the table has done it.
- Was **decapitated** at full health by two Munchkin blunderbusses firing together, and brought back in the same round when Snarfa spent his last two spell slots on her.
- Grew a mushroom thick enough to catch Bob's third fall. It is the only reason he lived through it.
- Withdrew to the College with the party. A passing Restoration student healed her and Alistrassa as an extra-credit demonstration.

**State at close:** Health Normal · Tokens 9 · Gold 20 (+20 in the group fund).

## Session Four (2026-09-24)

- Talked Munge into carrying a promise to the Don: Finnegan comes once the party has healed.
- Produced two looted blunderbusses from her skirt and gave one to Alistrassa. Abjuration's wards nearly took hers apart in her hands.
- Healed Snarfa after his first attempt at flying out of a window.
- Spent heavily on the party's read of the Hamlet job: not the Don, or not with his knowledge.
- Directed Snarfa's glamour on Finnegan, and asked for the name spell that left him mute.
- Traded Penny two books for abjuration runes on her parasol.

**State at close:** Health Normal · Tokens 4 · Gold 20 (+20 in the group fund).
