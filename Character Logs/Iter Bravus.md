Sheet: [Iter Bravus](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md).

## Session Zero (2026-08-29)

- Emerged from the caves under [New Squishnesta](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md) alongside [Bob](../Player%20Sheets/Baeon%20"Bob"%20Oberoni.md), having led blind Bob through the dark for days, following the Red Lady's vision — "find a man stumbling out of the depths of a cave."
- The Red Lady's message, clarified in play: she only ever said *find* the man, never *follow* him, and never spoke of a "path." Her actual guidance: "**It's about a place, darling. It's about a place.**"
- Became **aligned with the whole party** on a bonding roll. Her bonding vignette: got very high on cave mushrooms, connected with the Red Lady.
- The Red Lady / menstruation argument with [Lark](../Player%20Sheets/Lark%20Myca%20Roux.md): [Iter](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md) kept talking "to learn more," took disadvantage for the rest of that conversation, stayed aligned with Lark.
- Took the "tripping" condition from Lark's hotbox (elected to fail the save).
- Weakening as the moon wanes — the spore-druid phase mechanic surfaced.
- Parasol ruled a one-handed piercing melee weapon.
- In the [Finnegan](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md) fight: raised a tree-sized mushroom through the tavern floor under him (charisma 15 vs 8) — knocked him prone, one damage step; wrecked the floor.

## Session One (2026-09-02)

Sent [Finnegan](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md) off to pick a flower so the party could talk, and lost him to three Munchkin gangsters. Linked arms with [the Don](../NPC%20Sheets/Don%20Aldo%20Zuccaro.md)'s men on the road and drew their life stories out of them; took tea with [Ziggy](../NPC%20Sheets/Ziggy.md). Danced a drow "courtesy dance" to distract Don Zuccaro while [Lark](../Player%20Sheets/Lark%20Myca%20Roux.md) dosed him, and it worked. Healed [Munge](../NPC%20Sheets/Munge.md), the Don's half-giant, from critical to major after the raid. Ate [Nonna](../NPC%20Sheets/Nonna.md)'s candy spaghetti and regretted it (Constitution 1). Now a friend of the Zuccaro family.

**State at close:** Health Normal · Tokens 10 · Gold 20 (+20 in the group fund).

## Session Two (2026-09-09)

- Healed [Munge](../NPC%20Sheets/Munge.md) twice more with the spore cloud — once at [the safe house](../Lore/Places/The%20Don's%20Safe%20House.md), once on the road to [the depot](../Lore/Places/The%20Red%20Line%20Depot.md) — until he was nearly whole and could carry a club. Let [Alistrassa](../Player%20Sheets/Alistrassa.md) step into the cloud too; it eased her physical exhaustion (not her disease).
- Ran the distraction lock on **[Bartholomew Groundshaker](../NPC%20Sheets/Bartholomew%20Groundshaker.md)** at the depot gate: danced, flirted, offered water and mints. Her Constitution exploded (4+3), so he stayed charmed and oblivious for a very long time while the others raided the warehouse.
- On the plan to walk into the pirates and lie: rolled instinct 12 and read it plainly — "you're going to get absolutely fucking murdered; they'd just see you as an obstacle." The party hid instead.

**State at close:** Health Normal · Tokens 12 · Gold 20 (+20 in the group fund).

## Session Three (2026-09-16)

- Raised an eight-foot mushroom through the undercarriage of the departing train and **turned it over**, stopping [Finnegan](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md) leaving [the depot](../Lore/Places/The%20Red%20Line%20Depot.md).
- Scooped Finnegan out of the wreck with the parasol, and scooped him back again when he slipped loose.
- **Held her reaction** rather than spending it — the first time anyone at the table has done it.
- Was **decapitated** at full health by two Munchkin blunderbusses firing together, and brought back in the same round when [Snarfa](../NPC%20Sheets/Snarfa.md) spent his last two spell slots on her.
- Grew a mushroom thick enough to catch Bob's third fall. It is the only reason he lived through it.
- Withdrew to [the College](../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md) with the party. A passing [Restoration](../Lore/Places/Restoration.md) student healed her and [Alistrassa](../Player%20Sheets/Alistrassa.md) as an extra-credit demonstration.

**State at close:** Health Normal · Tokens 9 · Gold 20 (+20 in the group fund).

## Session Four (2026-09-24)

- Talked [Munge](../NPC%20Sheets/Munge.md) into carrying a promise to [the Don](../NPC%20Sheets/Don%20Aldo%20Zuccaro.md): [Finnegan](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md) comes once the party has healed.
- Produced two looted blunderbusses from her skirt and gave one to [Alistrassa](../Player%20Sheets/Alistrassa.md). [Abjuration](../Lore/Places/Abjuration.md)'s wards nearly took hers apart in her hands.
- Healed [Snarfa](../NPC%20Sheets/Snarfa.md) after his first attempt at flying out of a window.
- Spent heavily on the party's read of the [Hamlet](../Lore/Places/Hamlet.md) job: not the Don, or not with his knowledge.
- Directed Snarfa's glamour on Finnegan, and asked for the name spell that left him mute.
- Traded [Penny](../NPC%20Sheets/Penny.md) two books for abjuration runes on her parasol.

**State at close:** Health Normal · Tokens 4 · Gold 20 (+20 in the group fund).

## Session Five (2026-09-30)

- Handed [Seamus](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md)'s rope to [Pudge](../Player%20Sheets/Pudge.md).
- Read [Snarfa](../NPC%20Sheets/Snarfa.md) as hopelessly smitten with [Calliope](../NPC%20Sheets/Calliope.md).
- Led a drow dance through [the quad](../Lore/Places/The%20Quad.md); a large crowd followed her.
- Talked karma with [Alistrassa](../Player%20Sheets/Alistrassa.md). At fifty, she wonders if this is her first life.
- When [Ra](../NPC%20Sheets/Ra.md)'s beam went wrong, carried Alistrassa's words to Snarfa.
- Spotted the odd "student" in the Hawaiian shirt with Alistrassa (three tokens between them to beat the DC), and met [Ada Crane](../NPC%20Sheets/Ada%20Crane.md).
- Caught Snarfa when he stumbled, kissed him, and left with him. Calliope saw, and turned away; only [Iter](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md) noticed.

**State at close:** Health Minor · Tokens 5 · Gold 20 (+20 in the group fund).

## Session Six (2026-10-07)

- Took the parasol canvas from [Penny](../NPC%20Sheets/Penny.md), runed against physical harm, and sewed it in.
- Her mushroom brought [Pudge](../Player%20Sheets/Pudge.md) and [Seamus](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md) out of the [Brightwater](../Lore/Places/The%20River%20Squish.md) when the bridge dropped.

- Asked the thief if she was hurt. It landed; [Ember](../Player%20Sheets/Ember%20Spanner.md) cried, and the fight was over.
- Healed everyone with her spores in the library.
- Talked with the Red Lady off screen. The conversation is owed, one-on-one.

**State at close:** Health Normal · Tokens 4 · Gold 20 (+20 in the group fund).
