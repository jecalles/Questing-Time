## Session Zero (2026-08-29)

- First words of the campaign: "What the fuck am I doing here?"
- Lost in the caves with [Iter](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md), blind in the dark, praying to Yuhai; an "English divining rock" pointed them to the exit. Covered in Yuhai's ash from an explosion weeks ago; refuses to wash it off.
- **Yuhai manifested physically** from the ash during the all-night party — a well-meaning idiot "fully aligned with some higher power," who charged Bob to "lead the Bobs in the light of the moon." Bob scooped the settled ash into a vial he now wears.
- Aligned with Iter (bonding beat). Neutral with Lark.
- Picked up a rusty bladeless sword hilt in a scabbard and a half-filled spittoon. Still carrying two unread wax-sealed scrolls.
- In the Finnegan fight: released the moth (it stupefied Finnegan), grabbed him by the beard — then flipped to *protecting* him, tripped over his own rope, and body-slammed him (major damage, bleeding). The freed moth follows the party now.
- Recognised the Battered Drum barkeep as a fellow Yuhai follower. Knew **Shiny Bob** — an undead man in light-absorbing armour — long ago, and believed him dead.

## Session One (2026-09-02)

Suggested whacking the three gangsters and heard three safeties click off. Communed with the moon at camp while Lark set a glowing toadstool on his chest; the contact went sideways and physical, and afterwards he understood he had been reaching for Yuhai, not the Moon, and that his instinct had been amplified with neither one's discernment. Slept like the dead and woke rested. Rang the sacred cowbell to cover Lark's dosing of the Don. Stood up to his full six and a half feet, asked the Don for work, rolled a 15, and was sent to talk to Munge — which made the party friends of the Zuccaro family. Has not checked in with his College handler. Tommy Two Cheeks kissed him goodnight.

**State at close:** Health Normal · Tokens 2 · Gold 20 (+20 in the group fund).

## Session Two (2026-09-09)

- Beseeched **Yuhai** by moonlight, "with hubris." Both times the moon visibly **darkened** at Yuhai's name before a beam of moonlight lit the way to the depot. Late in the session he felt the moon darken and brighten again at the name — "some kind of passion happening here" between Yuhai and the Moon.
- Bonked Bartholomew on the head with a train cowbell (Prowess 7); the dwarf did not so much as flinch.
- Stripped off his white robe to go grey and sooty with the ash for camouflage.
- Snarfa cast **greater invisibility** on him — fully visible, near-silent, holds through an attack — and he leapt for Finnegan's train container. Botched the climb (Prowess 2 vs DC 4): **two broken fingers, minor damage**. Pudge's friendly die got him up and over; he dropped in quietly (15 with advantage).
- Poked the huddled figure inside — "sounds a lot like Finnegan" — and it woke and shouted before he could use Snarfa's knockout rune. Gunshots overhead. End of session.

## Session Three (2026-09-16)

- Lost Greater Invisibility to a bad Constitution roll while still inside the container with Finnegan.
- **Died twice.** Both times he stood before a towering white-robed figure — pink bulbous head, many eyes, six pairs of wings — holding golden scales with a single feather. Both times his heart was drawn out, weighed, found heavier, and he was thrown back. The **Moon** attended both, troubled the first time and disappointed the second; **Yuhai** attended the second and was deeply confused by it.
- Before the first death, unconscious, he reached for the Red Lady and the Moon together and was told by both to *fuck off, darling*.
- **Survived a third fall** only because Iter grew a mushroom under him. He got up with a broken spine.
- Woke **covered in sand** after the falls. It looked like the sand from the crate.
- Snorted sugar and sand, flung a spittoon into Halvard's eyes, glided down from the sky and killed a Munchkin at terminal velocity.
- At the College, Alistrassa read him through her lens and found **a Bob named on her karmic wasting list**. He recognised the name as his own and has no memory of it being written.

**State at close:** Health **Minor damage** (broken spine) · Tokens 3 · Gold 20 (+20 in the group fund).

## Session Four (2026-09-24)

- Rested to full from the broken spine.
- Recognised the blunderbuss from the Heart; nearly dropped it anyway.
- Told the elves about the moon goddess, the scales and the feather, and the sand and sugar before each death.
- Came back with mismatched ears.
- Saw the list: sixteen crossed out, a seventeenth circled, Bob.
- Proposed breaking into Dean Alistair's study. Now watching Seamus.

**State at close:** Health Normal · Tokens 5 · Gold 20 (+20 in the group fund).

## Session Five (2026-09-30)

- Cast his first spell: waved his hands the way Snarfa did, and a glowing blue dome came down over the couch. It stayed where he cast it and muffled everything at its edge. The party talked inside it.
- Met Ra, who called him brother and shed a single tear. Bob let himself be comforted, tense. Knows the Sun lights the Moon; kept it to himself.
- Moshed, drunk, into Ra's beam: Constitution 4 against 43. Burned away from the waist up. His third death.
- Stood before the figure with the scales again. His heart sank again; the figure wept, and the Moon held its head: "Darling, he'll get it one day." She told Bob it was a wonderful spell, that he asked nicely, and that he should do it more. Yuhai told him to cast for her majesty; she shook her head.
- Woke in the quad, naked and covered in sand, beside his own body: legs, and bare skeleton from the waist up. Sat there with Alistrassa.

**State at close:** Health Normal · Tokens 8 · Deaths 3 · Gold 20 (+20 in the group fund).

## Session Six (2026-10-07)

- Saw the runner in a moonbeam before the shadows pulled her out of it.
- Cast Speak with Animals on his moth, and the Moon answered on a 93. **Mothra** blinked through a crack in time and space into the thief's face, and Bob rode its senses.
- Pulled a tree out of the ground with Pudge, then held Ember with a grapple and a kind word.
- In the library, read how the planes are laid out and found a treatise, *Karmic Topology*. Advantage, for good, on rolls about the planes.
- Brushed his own sand off into a leather pouch. Dressed from the lost-and-found; everything is too small.

**State at close:** Health Normal · Tokens 8 (as at Session Five; not given at Session Six) · Deaths 3 · Gold 20 (+20 in the group fund).
