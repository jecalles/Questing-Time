## The side session (2026-09-06) — four months at the College

- **Built in conversation with the GM** as a high elf wizard with a karmic wasting disease: five years, no Celestial at the end, and innate casting that hurries it. Dice: Int D20, Wis D12, Ins D10, Cha D8, Pro D6, Con D4.
- **Day one.** Tim Aboul at the Restoration clinic ("it is not good"); Martha in procurement; the fairy at the dean's door; **Dean Alistair**, who told her plainly she would probably die and hired her to the karmic-restoration desk whose last holder had just died at it. Two cantrips granted on the spot: Identify, and comprehension.
- **The dead wizard's ledger:** twenty-three cases, all deceased, most high elves of her own chain, one "Bob."
- **Abjuration.** Snarfa at the desk; Penny in the writing room, stuck on the flick. Intro texts by chit. A two-week research project: turn her cantrips into runes so she stops paying for them. Took the two weeks rather than the karmic damage.
- **The project grew.** Glass, from Ellis by the Red Line; a light, from Jeff's tip and Snarfa's phone — the sun god Ra; ink that lets light through paper, from Eustace and Muriel; the dean's folio, used up on the way; a stage of heat-resistant pipe; Snarfa's spray-can wards. Two months became four.
- **The party.** Penny slid the inked folio into the slate; Ra shone in time with his own heartbeat; fifty-one lenses in a row, none wasted: one took the identify rune, fifty took their basis patterns, and the light burnt through fifty feet of floor on the way. She put it in her glasses and looked.
- **Aligned with Snarfa.** Ended present-day with the party, in New Squishnesta, employed, broke, and out of time.

**State at close:** Health Normal · Karma Normal · Tokens 2 · Gold 0.

## Session Two (2026-09-09) — she joins the party

- **Cold open at the College.** Writing in the margins of the dead wizard's ledger when **Dean Alistair** knocked with a rumour from Martha: adventurers new to town, carrying a College rune, "might have special importance." She read him as under-informed and pawning research onto her (Int 12), took a chit for a runes dictionary, and was sent to the Red Line — via Martha, who found the address (blue ink): "Pudge is either dead, or consorting with a known gangster."
- **The Don's manor.** Walked in on the party mid-morning. "Hi, I'm Alistrassa — you can just call me Alice." Pudge, seeing the robes not the face: "it is I whom you are looking for." The party pulled aside, read her as not nefarious, and both sides struck a deal: **"I will help you with this if you will help me with that."** She is now with the party — Neutral both ways, no onboarding mechanic.
- **The death rune, over dinner.** Did not look at it — an assassination rune reads back at whoever reads it, and she has no armour — but spoke to kill-runes in the abstract: transmutation; on paper that small, either a prodigious caster's work or a bare, unsafeguarded simple spell; a countersign is possible but needs a Dispel line, greater runecraft, and research she does not have.
- **Chits made her dizzy** (magic on top of exhaustion) — ruled stress, **not** karmic damage.
- **The depot.** Her lens ranked what mattered to her cure: the buried **sand** brightest of all, then Bramblebeard, then Finnegan, then Martha. Swept the warehouse and found the one glowing crate — third row in from the wall, four crates stacked on top of it. Reached for the velvet pouch — **Snarfa picked it up first and drew its rune out; it would have killed her.** Deferred a full Identify of the sand (it would speed her disease) to a safe version built at the College.

**State at close:** Health Normal · Karma Normal · Tokens 3 · Gold 0.

## Session Three (2026-09-16)

- **Shot in the jaw** at close range by a blunderbuss, which destroyed it.
- Asked Snarfa to get her out and was teleported **four hundred feet straight up**. She spent the rest of the session falling under a feather fall, out of range, calling observations down to the others.
- Lost her rune book and her pen in the air.
- At the College she went first to **Restoration**, her own department, and talked a passing student into healing her and Iter as an extra-credit demonstration.
- **Burned three tokens reading Bob through her lens** and was briefly blinded by how hard it lit. **A Bob is named on her karmic wasting list** — and everyone else on that list is already dead. Neither of them can explain it.

**State at close:** Health Minor damage · Karma Normal · Tokens 2 · Gold 0.

## Session Four (2026-09-24)

- Was handed one of Iter's looted blunderbusses; guessed its workings wrong.
- Showed Bob the book from under her predecessor's body.
- Concluded the College is laundering money: legal reagent buying through Procurement, at whatever the seller asks.
- Told the party, inside Snarfa's shield, that Dean Alistair sent her to them.
- Proposed the arm-and-resurrection plan and offered her department's budget.
- Found she has the runes for a lens-and-mirror projector; inscribing a cloak meanwhile.
- Asked Martha to look into the sand, as the start of a paper.

**State at close:** Health Normal · Karma Normal · Tokens 3 · Gold 0.

## Session Five (2026-09-30)

- Gave Penny the umbrella canvas, Iter's parasol and a cup of herbal tea. Penny is studying runic projection mapping.
- Remembered, with Pudge, that every magical writing she has seen is blue. The rune is black.
- Told Iter her theory: elves and drow come back as Illuminated beings, and a drow was probably a demon last time. Her own soul is rotting, so not her. Saved the notebook from a glass of wine.
- Planned a 23-rune light shell and built the stage with the mages over an hour. Ra fired before Calliope's last song: eleven runes cut.
- Saw the beam split between Restoration's spire and Conjuration's, over the dungeons: "This is not good." Asked Snarfa, through Iter, to stop Ra. He would not: "he just killed Bob."
- Walked a salt circle round a "student" in a Hawaiian shirt, spotted with Iter (three tokens between them). A demon's head later bounced inside it without end. Read the student, Ada Crane, as deceitful but not malicious.
- Sat with Bob beside his half-skeleton.

**State at close:** Health Minor · Karma Normal · Tokens 4 · Gold 0.
