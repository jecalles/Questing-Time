## Session Six (2026-10-07)

- Blew the road between [the College](../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md) and [the Castle](../Lore/Places/The%20Castle.md) and took [Ada Crane](../NPC%20Sheets/Ada%20Crane.md)'s ledgers from under a fallen beam.
- Dropped a bridge into the [Brightwater](../Lore/Places/The%20River%20Squish.md) behind her.
- When the party closed, her shadow rose up in her shape with a hammer and screamed. Everyone but [Pudge](../Player%20Sheets/Pudge.md) reeled.
- Slipped Pudge twice, dropped a lintel, and went up a tree. Pudge and Bob pulled it out of the ground. Mothra blinked into her face.
- Bob held her. [Iter](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md) asked if she was hurt, and she cried.
- Took them to an old schoolhouse library across the river, a place she trusts. Neutral with the party now.
- Read her mother's old book in a corner, and gave [Lark](../Player%20Sheets/Lark%20Myca%20Roux.md) an account of her mother's time in [the Sharp Forest](../Lore/Sharp%20Forest.md).

**State at close:** Health Normal · Tokens not given.
