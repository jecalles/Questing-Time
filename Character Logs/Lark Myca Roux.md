## Session Zero (2026-08-29)

- **Named at the table: Lark Myca Roux**, goes by Lark. Family: the Roux, "the Roux of Razor's Edge," diplomats — an untapped resource she refuses to call on because she is broke and won't hear "I told you so."
- Floated out of the Sharp Forest on the edge of Squishnestashire; her aura drops squirrels out of trees. Picked up **five squirrels** ("they have a drug problem"); off-screen they drank her gas charges dry and foraged reagents.
- Casting medium established: an enchanted liquid — vodka plus fragrant moss — that she treats as "mana."
- Met Finnegan on the way into New Squishnesta and got him high on her gases ("Finnegan, you're one of us"). Ended the session aligned with him.
- Aligned with Pudge (bonding beat) and with Iter (party-wide bond); Neutral with Bob.
- In the Finnegan fight: hit him pre-initiative with **Endless Threat of Distraction** (charisma 13 vs his constitution 6); missed a hose-trip; sent the squirrels in; dropped a smoke bomb to cover the retreat. Also used Hallucination earlier. Burned 2 common reagents.
- A Gary Busey (a "once thought mythical" creature) is the source of her largest collected tooth.

## Session One (2026-09-02)

Talked the party out of a firefight and into a deal with the Don's men. Found her truth-serum recipe in the scratch book that fell out of her coat (Pudge found the coat), foraged yellow-bellied frog sweat and a sprig of young yew, and got cinnamon out of Slo Mo's tea bag; the craft roll exploded to 27 and the brew came out **Greater** — four charges. Dosed Finnegan with a quarter vial and got the whole sad story; told him death is not the end. Converted a charge into her rig and dosed Don Zuccaro under cover of "allergies" while Iter danced. Collected three drops of morning dew. Now a friend of the Zuccaro family.

**State at close:** Health Normal · Tokens 2 · Gold 20 (+20 in the group fund).

## Between sessions (2026-09-06) — the leaving

- **Proposed to the Don:** thanks for the trust; he has questions about Finnegan's College connection and so does she; she'll go to the College and ask, if he funds the trip. A side quest for him and a side-side quest of her own.
- **Her own quest:** the Sour Patch mushroom, a candied mushroom she knew only by story, which does not grow where she is from — through the corner kids, whom she wants to understand, and to whom she means to offer a holistic methadone and a meditation in exchange for what they know of the supply. Granted as a greater item quest. The rune study beside it is a greater information quest.
- **Canon, ruled:** her people seed their dead into mushrooms and eat them to reach the ancestor; each mushroom reaches a different realm. Her mycology notebook is in the rig.
- **Left abruptly.** Letters from Lark begin: Letters from Lark/README.
