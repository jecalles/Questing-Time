## Session Zero (2026-08-29)

- **Named at the table: [Lark Myca Roux](../Player%20Sheets/Lark%20Myca%20Roux.md)**, goes by Lark. Family: the Roux, "the Roux of Razor's Edge," diplomats — an untapped resource she refuses to call on because she is broke and won't hear "I told you so."
- Floated out of the [Sharp Forest](../Lore/Sharp%20Forest.md) on the edge of [Squishnestashire](../Lore/Places/Squishnestashire.md); her aura drops squirrels out of trees. Picked up **five squirrels** ("they have a drug problem"); off-screen they drank her gas charges dry and foraged reagents.
- Casting medium established: an enchanted liquid — vodka plus fragrant moss — that she treats as "mana."
- Met [Finnegan](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md) on the way into [New Squishnesta](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md) and got him high on her gases ("Finnegan, you're one of us"). Ended the session aligned with him.
- Aligned with [Pudge](../Player%20Sheets/Pudge.md) (bonding beat) and with [Iter](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md) (party-wide bond); Neutral with Bob.
- In the Finnegan fight: hit him pre-initiative with **Endless Threat of Distraction** (charisma 13 vs his constitution 6); missed a hose-trip; sent the squirrels in; dropped a smoke bomb to cover the retreat. Also used Hallucination earlier. Burned 2 common reagents.
- A Gary Busey (a "once thought mythical" creature) is the source of her largest collected tooth.

## Session One (2026-09-02)

Talked the party out of a firefight and into a deal with [the Don](../NPC%20Sheets/Don%20Aldo%20Zuccaro.md)'s men. Found her truth-serum recipe in the scratch book that fell out of her coat ([Pudge](../Player%20Sheets/Pudge.md) found the coat), foraged yellow-bellied frog sweat and a sprig of young yew, and got cinnamon out of [Slo Mo](../NPC%20Sheets/Slo%20Mo.md)'s tea bag; the craft roll exploded to 27 and the brew came out **Greater** — four charges. Dosed [Finnegan](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md) with a quarter vial and got the whole sad story; told him death is not the end. Converted a charge into her rig and dosed Don Zuccaro under cover of "allergies" while [Iter](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md) danced. Collected three drops of morning dew. Now a friend of the Zuccaro family.

**State at close:** Health Normal · Tokens 2 · Gold 20 (+20 in the group fund).

## Between sessions (2026-09-06) — the leaving

- **Proposed to [the Don](../NPC%20Sheets/Don%20Aldo%20Zuccaro.md):** thanks for the trust; he has questions about [Finnegan](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md)'s College connection and so does she; she'll go to [the College](../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md) and ask, if he funds the trip. A side quest for him and a side-side quest of her own.
- **Her own quest:** the Sour Patch mushroom, a candied mushroom she knew only by story, which does not grow where she is from — through the corner kids, whom she wants to understand, and to whom she means to offer a holistic methadone and a meditation in exchange for what they know of the supply. Granted as a greater item quest. The rune study beside it is a greater information quest.
- **Canon, ruled:** her people seed their dead into mushrooms and eat them to reach the ancestor; each mushroom reaches a different realm. Her mycology notebook is in the rig.
- **Left abruptly**, with a goodbye note on her bed.

## Session Six (2026-10-07)

- Came back to [the College](../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md) in a long black cloak, squirrels round her neck. The squirrel left with the party fell drunk out of [Snarfa](../NPC%20Sheets/Snarfa.md)'s goblet and joined the chain.
- Named the squirrels: Larry, Curly, The Bald One I Think, Yes and No.
- Talked to the thief from the back of the chase. Catapulted a squirrel at her off a gas tank, and missed; the bald one took the worst of it.
- In the library, read the history and the genealogies of [the Sharp Forest](../Lore/Sharp%20Forest.md)'s peoples, and worked out that "karma's bent because karma's mushrooms". She reads karma by Instinct now, at advantage.
- [Ember](../Player%20Sheets/Ember%20Spanner.md) gave her a written account of her mother's time in the Sharp Forest, twenty years ago. [Lark](../Player%20Sheets/Lark%20Myca%20Roux.md) was honoured. They are Aligned.

**State at close:** Health Normal · Tokens 2 (on her return; not given at Session Six) · Gold 2 (+20 in the group fund).
