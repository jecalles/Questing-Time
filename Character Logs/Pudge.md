Sheet: [Pudge](../Player%20Sheets/Pudge.md).

## Session Zero (2026-08-29)

- Briefed in a College basement by a nervous old wizard and ordered to shadow the incoming party and make sure their quest succeeds — "[the College of Mages](../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md) is deeply interested in the results." Read the wizard as agitated and hiding something (Instinct 7). Then sat in [the Battered Drum](../Lore/Places/The%20Tavern.md) some 36 hours before the party arrived.
- **Opened the envelope** by his own choice. His armour absorbed a College death rune — two of his spines cracked; only he saw the paper; the whole party took minor damage. Went **misaligned with [Finnegan](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md)** on reading it, then **aligned (smitten)** after a natural-20 persuasion.
- The armour gives him unbroken memory through the shared table-vision the casters experience as a "trip" — he is privately tracking it as "a strange phenomenon."
- Serial **N0288/A** — the "/A" for "abnormal."

## Session One (2026-09-02)

Asked [Finnegan](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md) the practical questions (which way is [the College](../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md), which way is Bill) and got the geography. Found [Lark](../Player%20Sheets/Lark%20Myca%20Roux.md)'s recipe book in her coat. Kept quiet and watchful through [the Don](../NPC%20Sheets/Don%20Aldo%20Zuccaro.md)'s parlour; noticed the second half-giant in the corner — [Munge](../NPC%20Sheets/Munge.md), eight feet, long arms, "one of my kind" — and asked the Don how he came by him. Broke the back door down when the wet thuds started. At the end of the night, watching the Don sign in crimson, remembered that the death rune was written in **plain black ink**, and could not say why that mattered.

**State at close:** Health Normal · Tokens 7 · Gold 20 (+20 in the group fund).

## Session Two (2026-09-09)

- Named his handler at last: **[Mark Thornbush](../NPC%20Sheets/Mark%20Thornbush.md)** — the small wizard from [the College](../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md) basement. Dictated a report chit to him ("still following [Finnegan](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md)… got side-quested… please advise") and got no reply all session. The College has gone quiet on him, which he finds strange.
- Re-examined the death rune he had already survived: a quarto sheet, a plain and simple hand "unlike any magic he has seen written down," and a third detail he could not place.
- At [the depot](../Lore/Places/The%20Red%20Line%20Depot.md), built the surrounding crates into a climbable staircase with [Munge](../NPC%20Sheets/Munge.md) (Prowess 10 + Munge 12) to get at the one glowing crate — third row in from the wall, four more stacked on top of it.
- Spotted the pirates moving in on a Constitution check (17, DC 10) while the rest of the party missed it.
- Added a friendly die to Bob's botched climb into Finnegan's train container.

## Session Three (2026-09-16)

- Took sustained blunderbuss fire and [Halvard Ironspur](../NPC%20Sheets/Halvard%20Ironspur.md)'s twin curved swords with minimal damage, and put Halvard down.
- **Lifted a wrecked train car** with the man who had been shooting at him still inside it.
- Re-grappled and re-tied **[Finnegan](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md)** after he got a hand loose.
- **Wrote "yes and yes" to [Mark Thornbush](../NPC%20Sheets/Mark%20Thornbush.md)'s chit and could not send it.** He is not magical; the paper fell on the ground, where [Iter](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md) and [Alistrassa](../Player%20Sheets/Alistrassa.md) read it. [The College](../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md) has heard nothing back and is still waiting.
- Withdrew to the College with Finnegan bound and critical.

**State at close:** Health Normal · Tokens 7 · Gold 20 (+20 in the group fund).

## Session Four (2026-09-24)

- Away (Alyssa absent). Went to answer [Mark Thornbush](../NPC%20Sheets/Mark%20Thornbush.md)'s chit in person.

**State at close:** unchanged — Health Normal · Tokens 7 · Gold 20 (+20 in the group fund).

## Session Five (2026-09-30)

- Went down to the [Groundskeeping](../Lore/Places/Groundskeeping.md) office. Wardens' placards hang on hooks along the hallway; his is one of them.
- Lied to [Mark Thornbush](../NPC%20Sheets/Mark%20Thornbush.md) for the first time: [Finnegan](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md) slipped away in the [Hamlet](../Lore/Places/Hamlet.md) crowd, and the envelope with him. Thornbush believed him — [Pudge](../Player%20Sheets/Pudge.md) "would never tell a lie."
- Was given the same order four times: get the envelope to Finnegan, confirm he has it, report back, and ideally have him open it in the Hamlet tavern. The reward: a Saturday off and a ticket to Seaside. "This is the first time you failed me in years."
- Left feeling sick. Noticed that Thornbush insisted Finnegan receive the envelope, and cared less whether he opened it.
- Counted three placards left on the stairs: his own, N0008, and N0241 with a red X through it. Will think on it later.
- Found the envelope in his own coat. He had it all along.
- Had the foresight to stop the party talking in the open (19 on a D8). Bob's dome went up, and he told them everything inside it.
- Knew Hamlet for what it is: no police, three deaths in three years, all natural. A murder there gets talked about, not investigated.
- Remembered, with [Alistrassa](../Player%20Sheets/Alistrassa.md), that every magical writing in his life has been blue. The rune is black.
- Took Seamus's rope from [Iter](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md) and carries him like a handbag. Noticed [Snarfa](../NPC%20Sheets/Snarfa.md) sings a little too well. Met [Ada Crane](../NPC%20Sheets/Ada%20Crane.md).

**State at close:** Health Normal · Tokens 5 · Gold 20 (+20 in the group fund).

## Session Six (2026-10-07)

- Lifted the beam off [Ada Crane](../NPC%20Sheets/Ada%20Crane.md) after the road blew.
- Went into the [Brightwater](../Lore/Places/The%20River%20Squish.md) with [Seamus](../NPC%20Sheets/Seamus.md) when the second charge dropped the bridge. [Iter](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md)'s mushroom brought them out.
- His armour held off the Shade's scream; everyone else reeled.
- Grappled the thief twice and could not hold her. Grinned at her awkwardly.
- Pulled a tree out of the ground with Bob, with her in it. Took a knock from the fall.
- Curled up to sleep in the library the way he always does: three circles, then the floor.

**State at close:** Health Normal · Tokens 4 · Gold 20 (+20 in the group fund).
