## Session Zero (2026-08-29)

- Briefed in a College basement by a nervous old wizard and ordered to shadow the incoming party and make sure their quest succeeds — "the College of Mages is deeply interested in the results." Read the wizard as agitated and hiding something (Instinct 7). Then sat in the Battered Drum some 36 hours before the party arrived.
- **Opened the envelope** by his own choice. His armour absorbed a College death rune — two of his spines cracked; only he saw the paper; the whole party took minor damage. Went **misaligned with Finnegan** on reading it, then **aligned (smitten)** after a natural-20 persuasion.
- The armour gives him unbroken memory through the shared table-vision the casters experience as a "trip" — he is privately tracking it as "a strange phenomenon."
- Serial **N0288/A** — the "/A" for "abnormal."

## Session One (2026-09-02)

Asked Finnegan the practical questions (which way is the College, which way is Bill) and got the geography. Found Lark's recipe book in her coat. Kept quiet and watchful through the Don's parlour; noticed the second half-giant in the corner — Munge, eight feet, long arms, "one of my kind" — and asked the Don how he came by him. Broke the back door down when the wet thuds started. At the end of the night, watching the Don sign in crimson, remembered that the death rune was written in **plain black ink**, and could not say why that mattered.

**State at close:** Health Normal · Tokens 7 · Gold 20 (+20 in the group fund).

## Session Two (2026-09-09)

- Named his handler at last: **Mark Thornbush** — the small wizard from the College basement. Dictated a report chit to him ("still following Finnegan… got side-quested… please advise") and got no reply all session. The College has gone quiet on him, which he finds strange.
- Re-examined the death rune he had already survived: a quarto sheet, a plain and simple hand "unlike any magic he has seen written down," and a third detail he could not place.
- At the depot, built the surrounding crates into a climbable staircase with Munge (Prowess 10 + Munge 12) to get at the one glowing crate — third row in from the wall, four more stacked on top of it.
- Spotted the pirates moving in on a Constitution check (17, DC 10) while the rest of the party missed it.
- Added a friendly die to Bob's botched climb into Finnegan's train container.
