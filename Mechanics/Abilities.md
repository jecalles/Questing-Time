Abilities for [Questing Time!](../Lore/Questing%20Time!%20A%20Primer.md). Rules: [Questing Time! Mechanics](../Mechanics/Questing%20Time!%20Mechanics.md). Stat blocks: [Stat Blocks](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md).

Abilities modify a stat block. They may be permanent (true growth) or temporary (a condition). Capture character flaws and strengths as abilities.

# Racial Abilities

## Faerie's Grace

Roll Prowess or Instinct for all reactive rolls. This covers every reactive roll, not just Constitution.

Holder: [Fey (Seelie)](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#fey-seelie).

## Trickster

Roll all deception rolls as Planned Actions and with advantage.

Holder: [Fey (Seelie)](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#fey-seelie).

## Night Vision

The holder sees perfectly in the dark. No light source, and no roll.

Bright light costs the holder their perception. Before any check or reaction that depends on seeing or perceiving the environment, the holder first rolls Constitution. On a failure, they make that check at disadvantage. See [Dice and Rolls](../Mechanics/Questing%20Time!%20Mechanics.md#dice-and-rolls).

The Constitution roll is not a check in its own right, and it carries no penalty of its own. It only decides whether the check that follows is rolled at disadvantage.

[Sunglasses](../Mechanics/Items%20and%20Equipment.md#sunglasses) cancel the bright-light penalty outright.

This holds for every Drau, with no exception. Cave-dwelling and city-dwelling alike.

Holder: [Drau](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#drau).

## Sweet Blood

Sugar, nutmeg, and mad honey hit a Munchkin's body far harder than they hit anyone else's. A dose that barely registers on a human — a dessert, a sweetened drink — is enough to sedate, stimulate, or intoxicate a Munchkin outright. See [Substances](../Mechanics/Items%20and%20Equipment.md#substances).

This only triggers for a character carrying Munchkin blood: one or more parents or grandparents Munchkin. A Fey character may perceive these substances differently, but takes no mechanical effect from them.

The same blood runs the other way against most psychedelic compounds — Munchkins resist them, for reasons nobody has traced yet. This half of the ability stays undefined until a story goes looking for why.

Holder: [The Munchkin](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#the-munchkin).

## Ancient Instinct

Illuminated creatures sense the true alignment of anyone near them, passively and without a roll from the sensing creature. No hidden knowledge, no need to investigate.

When alignment matters for a check against an Illuminated creature, and the target is trying to hide or fake their standing, roll Charisma against the target's Instinct, the same pattern as other Gifted checks.

Non-Illuminated creatures can fake their alignment through deception or subterfuge, and fool an ordinary alignment-based check. Illuminated creatures cannot be fooled this way.

Holders: [High Elves](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#high-elves); [Fey (Seelie)](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#fey-seelie); [Devils](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#devils); [Demons](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#demons). It will hold for Celestials too, once a Celestial block is written.

# City Dweller Abilities

Traits that key to where a character was raised, not to their race. Any race may hold these.

## Smoothtalker

Strangers are not a problem for a city dweller.

The holder ignores the default Misaligned tier. They start at **Neutral** with any faction they have no history with. See [Alignment](../Mechanics/Questing%20Time!%20Mechanics.md#alignment).

This does not apply to Enemy or Ally standing. Those tiers are earned, and the holder earns them like anyone else.

Optional second clause, if you want the ability to do more: the holder may ask the GM a character's alignment tier before they act on it.

This is not a racial ability, and it is not tied to the Drau.

Holders: [City Dwellers](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#city-dwellers).

# Character Abilities

## Well Connected

Roll advantage for social checks involving people the character may know.

Holders: [Bob (name TBD)](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#bob-name-tbd).

## Reckless

Roll **proactive checks with advantage** — and every proactive check made **against** this character is rolled with advantage too. Always on; not a choice. The character does not know how to do it any other way.

## Rooted

Roll 2x dice for checks performed on familiar territory. Sum the dice, and let each of them explode.

Holders: [Slo Mo](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#slo-mo), [Bill the Butcher](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#bill-the-butcher), [Ellis](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#ellis).

## Magical Dampening

Any spell cast within the holder's own zone — friendly or enemy — is fully abjured, unless the holder has deliberately attuned to that caster first (e.g. a teammate). A spell cast from one zone away instead requires the caster to pass a DC 10 proactive spell check to land at all.

**Drawback.** The holder cannot be healed by magic while in combat. Removing the armor/rune, or a long rest, is required before magic can heal them outside combat.

**Exceptions.** The dampening field only stops spells moving through the air around the holder — it does nothing against magic that enters the body directly. Drunk or ingested potions, and magical or poison-tipped arrows, affect the holder normally.

Holder: [Pudge](../Player%20Sheets/Pudge.md).

## Beefy

Major damage is treated as Minor damage on the holder's Health track — this delays them reaching Critical. May require a Prowess check to fit through small spaces. Advantage on Intimidation checks.

**Racial:** Beefy is a **Giant and Half-Giant** racial ability — see [Giants](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#giants) — not just Pudge's personal one.

Holders: [Giants](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#giants), [Half-Giants](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#half-giants); [Pudge](../Player%20Sheets/Pudge.md).

## Fairy Wings

Flight. The holder can fly, or simply hover a few feet off the ground instead of walking. No roll required under normal circumstances.

Holder: [Robyn](../Player%20Sheets/Lark%20Myca%20Roux.md).

## Fairy Evasiveness

Granted as a drawback offset for a low Constitution die paired with carrying no weapon. Sourced from the holder's tattoos.

**Mechanics.** Roll Instinct or Prowess, holder's choice, instead of Constitution on reactive physical-defense checks. Robyn's own build uses Instinct.

Holder: [Robyn](../Player%20Sheets/Lark%20Myca%20Roux.md).

## Fairy Prestidigitation

A minor, optional flourish — a small shower of sparkle, a faint chime, or both, at the holder's discretion. Cosmetic only, no mechanical effect.

"Sometimes you're not in the mood to ding, and you don't ding." — GM.

Holder: [Robyn](../Player%20Sheets/Lark%20Myca%20Roux.md).

## Friend of the Forest

Roll Charisma to befriend a nearby creature as a companion. Tiered by the creature's power:

- **Minor** (small doves, sentient toadstools) and **Lesser** — trivially available given a high Charisma die.
- **Common** and **Greater** — require a narrative beat to unlock.
- **Major** — forest-scale companionship (the Sharp Forest itself is a Major Aspect of Gaia). Requires regional attunement plus a developed narrative arc, not a single beat.

**Mechanics for a bonded companion in play are not yet written** — this is an advancement track, not a finished ability.

Holder: [Robyn](../Player%20Sheets/Lark%20Myca%20Roux.md).

## Flow

A sustained buff state. Designed to generalize across Fae as they rise through the Karmic Order (Common → Greater → Major → Majestic) — [Robyn](../Player%20Sheets/Lark%20Myca%20Roux.md) is the first character to hold it, at Common tier.

**While active:** upcast all magic one level at no cost, and roll Prowess or Instinct in place of any reactive stat.

**Cost.** Every turn spent in Flow, roll Constitution, Wisdom, or Instinct against a DC that climbs with damage taken and how chaotic the scene is, GM's discretion. This is called the "Flow DC" and applies to any character using the ability, not just Robyn.

**Scaling, unwritten.** Greater/Major/Majestic Fae are meant to get a stronger version — a wider radius, advantage on the sustain check, or a higher base effect were all floated, but which upgrade belongs to which tier has not been decided. Treat this as a placeholder until a second Fae character, or a tier-up, forces the question.

## Open Book

At the start of each encounter with the holder, roll a d4 (exploding) to set available charges for that encounter only.

When a player rolls Instinct to detect emotional deception ("Insight"), the roll happens normally — but the holder may spend one charge to force the result to register as the target being truthful and earnest, regardless of the actual roll or the actual truth.

Charges do not carry over between encounters. Leaving and re-entering resets them, and the holder rerolls — possibly lower.

Wisdom checks against factual inconsistency are unaffected by this ability, and may still succeed. The holder rarely gives a player anything to catch that way. A successful Wisdom check does nothing mechanical — it's pure player-facing suspicion, narratively real but with no sheet effect. [The Don](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#don-aldo-zuccaro) holds it openly — on a man that old, answering honestly and telling you nothing is the joke. It may be assigned to other characters later.

## Hubrisy

For all checks, roll 2x dice pools. The GM secretly rolls a **d4** to set the luck: **odd = disadvantage, even = advantage** — your two pools resolve as that. This holds for any die size. You are never told the outcome of the luck roll.

**Crit-scale rolls.** For a rare high-stakes check rolled on a die larger than a d4 (e.g. a d100 to commune with a higher power), the GM may swap the d4 for wild results on the extremes of that die instead — a natural max or min triggering an outsized narrative outcome. GM's discretion; not a standing rule.

Spelled **"Hubrisy"**, not "Hubris."

## Fungible

If your character dies, you may continue the Game with a new character with identical stats and an adjusted Narrative at the GMs discression.

Holder: [Baeon "Bob" Oberoni](../Player%20Sheets/Baeon%20"Bob"%20Oberoni.md).

## Wounded 
Your character has Disadvantage on rolls of a particular stat or pertaining to a perticular action (dependent on type of wound, specified in each Character Sheet).

## Karmic Wasting

A condition, not a gift. The character's karma is rotting: innate casting hurries it (roll Constitution or take physical damage), hurrying a task by casting costs a point of karmic damage per action, and at the end of the track there is no return. See [Karmic Damage](../Mechanics/Questing%20Time!%20Mechanics.md#karmic-damage). Only [Alistrassa](../Player%20Sheets/Alistrassa.md) carries it.

## Intimidating 
Advantage on Intimidation checks.

## Luck of the Coin 
Narratively your character finds money in unexpected places. After finding money, the die size for one of your character's stats is demoted (GMs choice which and how much) until the end of the encounter.

## Unarmed Strikes

Removes the disadvantage an unarmed attack otherwise rolls with (see [Combat](../Mechanics/Questing%20Time!%20Mechanics.md#combat)) — the holder rolls Prowess normally when fighting bare-handed. Granted only where the character is narratively expected to fight well unarmed: a trained brawler, a martial artist, someone raised to it. Not a default; most characters stay at disadvantage unarmed.

## Evasive

Advantage on reactive checks to avoid damage. A lifetime of leaving rooms before anyone noticed, turned into a habit the body keeps.

**Shared with the holder's familiar.** Where the holder has a companion that mirrors them — see [The Shade](#the-shade) — that companion holds this ability too, and it upgrades for both at once along [Shade Advancement](#shade-advancement).

Holder: Ember Spanner.

## Shy

Disadvantage on Charisma checks against anyone the holder is **Misaligned** with (see [Alignment](../Mechanics/Questing%20Time!%20Mechanics.md#alignment)).

Narratively this reads as unfamiliarity, not timidity — the holder is fluent with her own people and lost with everyone else. Mechanically it keys off Alignment, which means it lifts as standing is earned rather than at the GM's discretion, and it starts near-total: every character begins Misaligned with every faction.

**This is what a d20 Charisma costs her.** The stat is her magic and her camouflage; it is not a licence to talk anyone into anything. Designed jointly with the player.

Holder: Ember Spanner.

## Tinkering

Given sufficient material components, the holder can build **common items** — simple traps, simple weapons, and comparable workshop goods. Planned Actions only; this needs hands, tools, and time.

**Traps may be built to type** — smoke, explosive, frost, poison — where the holder has the components for it. **Magical traps carry a higher DC** than mundane ones. Those DCs ease as the holder's tinkering improves.

**Acquired, not innate.** The GM grants this after a short narrative beat of the holder remembering the workshop she grew up in — roughly five minutes at the table, and eligible to happen during Session Zero. It supersedes the narrower "Skilled at Mending" it grew out of.

Intended holder: Ember Spanner, who does not hold it yet.

## The Shade

Ember Spanner's familiar. **A lesser aspect of [the Warden](../Lore/Magic%20System.md#the-warden)**, wearing the shape of the shadow it took from her. It is not a piece of Ember, and it is not the Warden in full — it is the Warden's presence scaled down to sit beside one person, granted under the pact so that she would not travel unprotected.

This also explains its manner. The Warden went True Neutral to enter Gaia (see [The Warden](../Lore/Magic%20System.md#the-warden)), and a True Neutral being does not hold virtues the way it used to. The Shade is protective without being kind.

**It works by inference, not instruction.** Ember has rarely asked it for anything, so it acts on what it has seen her do and what it knows of her mother. Told only "help," it does the most Shade-like thing available. The player's model for it is No-Face from *Spirited Away*.

All Shade powers are Gifted magic: Charisma against the target's Instinct.

**Stat block.** **The Shade shares the holder's stat block** rather than carrying its own. When the Shade rolls, it rolls the holder's die for that stat. Revisit if it ever needs to diverge.

**Idle:** watches from the shadows — the holder, the party, and the surrounding environment. **Visibility:** most visible in shadow; incorporeal in normal light; forced to retreat to the nearest shadow in powerful light. Advantage in darkness, vulnerable to light magic — clerics especially. **At rest:** the holder can talk to it. It is never wrong, and never kind — it will withhold or obscure the truth at its whim.

**If the Shade is KO'd, the holder goes Under immediately** (see The Drowse — the reworked Drowse rules live in Lily's unmerged hand-off diff, not yet folded into Ember's sheet).

Holder: Ember Spanner.

### Mirror Manifestation

A shadowy mirror of the holder. Distraction, hallucination, something to spook enemies, or a scout, as long as there is ample shadow. In combat it has the holder's basic abilities, and it shares the holder's [Evasive](#evasive).

**Ruling, 2026-08-17 (closes the d4-Constitution / shared-damage question on Player Sheets/Ember Spanner).** Damage sharing is intended, not a hole — it is the deliberate cost of a companion that can, at higher tiers, dodge or ghost a hit entirely. Base state: any damage done to the Shade is also done to the holder.

**Ruling, 2026-08-19 — sharing survives the Goad.** Pulling attacks onto a damage-sharing companion looks self-defeating, and it is not: the Shade is harder to hit than the holder, because it shares [Evasive](#evasive) and that shared evasion upgrades over play. The Goad trades a certain hit on a d4 Constitution for a contested one against advantage. See [Shade Advancement](#shade-advancement).

### Goad

The Shade makes itself the obvious threat. Charisma against the target's Instinct; on a success, the target directs its next attack at the Shade instead of its intended target.

This is the holder's aggro tool, and the reason a d4-Constitution character can hold a front line at all.

### Shade Attack

The Shade attacks in its own right, rolling the holder's Prowess (see **Stat block**, above).

### Paralysis

One target cannot move or push air out to shout. Charisma against their Instinct. Brief.

### Transposition / Switch Places

The holder and the Shade trade positions, across zones. Escape, ambush, or getting someone else out. **Proposed:** costs the holder's action, not their move.

**Note, 2026-08-19.** The Shade has already done this once, unprompted — it moved Ember into shadow to escape pursuit in [New Squishnesta](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md) before she had any idea it could. Treat that as a preview of this power, not a separate ability.

### Shade Advancement

**Ruling, 2026-08-17**, revised 2026-08-19 after the Lily follow-up interview. Answers how a melee character with a d4 Constitution survives — the Shade is the mitigation, unlocked over play rather than granted all at once. Milestone-driven, same as the rest of Ember's Advancement track.

**The 2026-08-19 revision moved evasion down to the base tier.** [Evasive](#evasive) is now a starting ability, shared with the Shade, so the track scales that shared evasion rather than introducing it.

- **Base (current).** The Shade shares damage 1:1 with the holder, per [Mirror Manifestation](#mirror-manifestation). It holds [Goad](#goad) and [Shade Attack](#shade-attack), and shares the holder's [Evasive](#evasive).
- **Tier 2 — harder still.** The shared evasion improves beyond advantage — the Shade becomes genuinely difficult to land a hit on, cutting how often shared damage triggers at all, without changing what happens when it does land.
- **Tier 3 — ethereal.** The Shade gains a number of Ethereal charges per encounter (count TBD). Spending one on an incoming hit fully negates the damage — the attack ghosts through, no shared damage — while the Shade keeps its evasion. High ceiling, deliberately: GM intent is a well-designed ability that scales, not a one-time fix.

Exact trigger conditions for each tier (which story beat, which milestone) are not yet pinned down — treat as a placeholder track until a session forces the question, matching [Flow](#flow)'s scaling note.

**Still owed:** the relationship mechanic. Lily's stated arc is that the bond should be **debilitating or rewarding** depending on whether Ember builds a healthy relationship with the Shade — "if she sticks to the shadows, then she becomes nothing." The GM approved the direction on 2026-08-19 because relationship maintenance is what Gifted magic already is. Undesigned as of this writing.

Holder: Ember Spanner.

## Beginner's Luck

Once per session, after seeing the result, the holder may reroll one failed check and take the second result. The holder never knows this is happening: to her, and to everyone watching, it reads as an improbable break.

## Vicious Grace

The holder may use reactions as actions and actions as reactions, gets **two reactions per turn** rather than one, and may cast **any spell** as a reaction.

## Wise like the Devil

Roll Wisdom in place of Intelligence or Instinct on any check. The holder does not out-think anyone or out-feel them. He simply already knows.

# Spells

Spells for [Questing Time!](../Lore/Questing%20Time!%20A%20Primer.md). Casting rules: [Casting](../Mechanics/Questing%20Time!%20Mechanics.md#casting).

Spells are a subset of Abilities. A character can cast only if they hold the matching casting ability below. Spell slots are themselves treated as abilities and are granted the same way — through story beats, at the GM's discretion.

Each spell entry should state:

- **Source**: Innate, Derived, or Gifted.
- **School**: per the affinity table in [Schools of Magic](../Lore/Magic%20System.md#schools-of-magic).
- **Components**: Material, Somatic, Verbal (any combination).
- **Action**: standard action, Ritual (Planned Action), or Planned Action for material components.
- **Range**: the reach in zones, per [Ranged Combat](../Mechanics/Questing%20Time!%20Mechanics.md#ranged-combat). Omit it for a spell that only ever touches the caster or an adjacent ally.
- **Effect**: what the spell does at cantrip level.
- **Upcast**: whether spending a higher slot adds dice to the casting check pool, or advances the stated effect. State which.

## Casting Abilities

Holding one of these abilities is what allows a character to cast at all. Multiple may be held by the same character.

### Sorcery

Grants casting access to Innate magic. Roll Prowess to cast; targets resist with Constitution.

### Wizardry

Grants casting access to Derived magic. Roll Intelligence to cast; targets resist with Wisdom.

### Gifted Channel

Grants casting access to Gifted magic. Roll Charisma to cast; targets resist with Instinct.

A Gifted caster does not analyze a spell. They ask for it — "the spirit of the forest comes upon me, and I wish this spell to go away," or "Red Lady, give me strength." This decides how a Gifted spell is classified. The patron's intent sets the school, not what the spell looks like. A mushroom is what [Fungal Shield](#fungal-shield) looks like; protection is what the patron wants; the patron's wish is what makes it Abjuration.

## Cantrips

### Healing Spore Cloud

- **Source**: Gifted.
- **School**: Restoration. Aligned for Gifted — the center-cut case for this source. See [Schools of Magic](../Lore/Magic%20System.md#schools-of-magic).
- **Components**: Verbal or Somatic — either alone is enough. No material component, so the spell does not force a Planned Action. A bound caster (arms restrained, mouth free) casts through Verbal; a gagged caster (mouth restrained, arms free) casts through Somatic. Bound and gagged together, they cannot cast it.
- **Action**: Action only, once per turn. It **cannot** be cast as a reaction.
- **Range**: Two zones. See [Ranged Combat](../Mechanics/Questing%20Time!%20Mechanics.md#ranged-combat).
- **Effect**: A cloud of spores bursts out around a point. Every friendly target in the radius heals one step on the Health track. Duration is one turn of combat at entry level.
- **Upcast**: Advances the effect, not the dice pool. Duration grows first — more turns of combat, and eventually permanent. A later add-on deals 1 damage per step to enemies in the radius, which makes the spell AoE damage as well as healing.

The spell costs no spell slot. Treat it as a cantrip for now.

Growth runs on two tracks at once: stronger versions of the cantrip that still burn no slot, and true upcasting that spends one. Which upgrade sits on which track is not decided.

The spell is ranged, not touch. That was a deliberate trade. A touch version would heal harder and clear status effects, but it would put the caster in melee — see the D4 Constitution on [Iter Bravus](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md).

**Healing scales with the party's attunement.** The spell heals harder as the party grows closer to the caster. This is a growth axis of its own, separate from the duration upcast above, and it is earned in play rather than bought. It suits the source: Gifted magic is relationship maintenance. See [Pantheon](../Lore/Magic%20System.md#pantheon).

**Enemy alignment does not matter here.** The spell targets friends, so where the enemies stand is simply not a consideration for the healing. This closes what was an open question. Once the damage add-on is taken, alignment does apply — at that point the spell targets enemies too. For the general rule on alignment and Gifted magic, see [Alignment](../Mechanics/Questing%20Time!%20Mechanics.md#alignment).

Holder: [Iter Bravus](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md).

### Hallucination Gas

- **Source**: Gifted.
- **School**: Transmutation (Illusion). Misaligned for Gifted — this is a real contested roll, not an easy win, which matches the affinity table. See [Schools of Magic](../Lore/Magic%20System.md#schools-of-magic).
- **Components**: Material — gas charges, three to start. Charges are consumed on use; refilled through foraging (a Charisma check, trivial for common reagents within the forest domain — see [Robyn's](../Player%20Sheets/Lark%20Myca%20Roux.md) attunement notes).
- **Action**: Not yet pinned down — treat as Action only until a session forces the reaction question, matching [Healing Spore Cloud](#healing-spore-cloud).
- **Range**: Cone, two zones. See [Ranged Combat](../Mechanics/Questing%20Time!%20Mechanics.md#ranged-combat).
- **Effect**: A gas cone bursts out from the caster. Each target rolls Constitution against the caster's Charisma. On a loss, the target is Tripping — hallucinating badly enough that they must pass a Wisdom or Constitution check, target's choice, before any action that depends on perceiving the world correctly. Tripping targets roll disadvantage against Persuasion or Deception checks.
- **Upcast**: Not yet decided — likely advances the effect (duration of Tripping, or a wider cone) rather than the dice pool, matching the pattern of the other Gifted cantrips here, but the GM has not confirmed which.

**Casting stat.** Charisma against the target's Constitution, as written above — Robyn casts off Charisma across the board, druid casting.

**Crafting, generalised 2026-08-27.** The material-component-plus-foraging model here is how Robyn's whole gas-and-powder kit works. From day one she can make the level-one version of any such ability she has access to — potentially all of them — but she carries none pre-made. Making one costs a story beat: she says what she is making, sources the parts in the fiction (the GM is lax — usually a quick stat roll and a short scene, a few charges out), and justifies how she knows the recipe. Common recipes she just knows; stronger ones may need a book, practice, or a conversation. Rarer components gate the stronger versions, keyed to how affiliated she is with the region. She starts Session Zero with zero finished consumables. See [Lark Myca Roux](../Player%20Sheets/Lark%20Myca%20Roux.md).

**Casting medium, loosened.** The delivery of these gases and powders runs on ordinary materials, and the bar is low — many common items work as a medium (a spirit like vodka, fragrant moss, a rag, a bottle). Robyn is rarely stopped for lack of a medium; treat it as flavour for how a cast looks, not a resource gate.

Holder: [Robyn](../Player%20Sheets/Lark%20Myca%20Roux.md).

### Endless Threat of Distraction

- **Source**: Gifted.
- **School**: Transmutation (Illusion). Misaligned for Gifted — a real contested roll, same as [Hallucination Gas](#hallucination-gas).
- **Components**: Material — a prepared gas/powder charge from her kit, on the same crafting-and-foraging model as [Hallucination Gas](#hallucination-gas) (see the Crafting note there, and the loosened casting-medium note).
- **Action**: Action. Used pre-initiative in play against a fleeing target — treat that as GM latitude on a reaction against someone leaving the scene, not a standing reaction option, until a session forces the question.
- **Range**: Cone / drifting fog, two zones.
- **Effect**: A fog bursts from the caster. Each target rolls Constitution against the caster's Charisma. On a loss the target **loses its current objective** — its thoughts cascade into unrelated things and it cannot hold a purpose ("I must be running, running, running…"). The target is also **Tripping**, exactly as [Hallucination Gas](#hallucination-gas) defines it (Wisdom or Constitution check, target's choice, before any action that depends on perceiving correctly; disadvantage against Persuasion or Deception). Duration one turn at entry level; it lingers on a target nobody snaps out of it.
- **Upcast**: Advances the effect — longer duration, or a wider fog — not the dice pool, matching the other cantrips here.

The overlap with Hallucination Gas's Tripping is deliberate: this spell adds the objective-loss on top of it. Selected from Robyn's proposed mind-magic list and cast twice at Session Zero. *Intrusive Thoughts* and the reworked "dark vapor" from the same list are still unselected.

Holder: [Robyn](../Player%20Sheets/Lark%20Myca%20Roux.md).

### Commune with Ancestors

- **Source**: Gifted.
- **School**: Divination. Aligned for Gifted — the center-cut case for this source, same as [Healing Spore Cloud](#healing-spore-cloud). See [Schools of Magic](../Lore/Magic%20System.md#schools-of-magic).
- **Components**: Material and Somatic — a mushroom-trip ritual that carries the caster into the [Feywild](../Lore/Magic%20System.md#the-feywild), where Fae dead go. Forces a Planned Action; this is not something cast mid-fight.
- **Action**: Ritual (Planned Action).
- **Range**: Self.
- **Effect**: The caster communes with ancestors — and, per the 2026-08-14 interview, potentially a wider range of beings tied to the same matter/karmic lineage — for knowledge, advice, or perspective. Treat consulted ancestors as advisors the GM can voice, not as a mechanical bonus in its own right.
- **Upcast**: Not yet decided.

Holder: [Robyn](../Player%20Sheets/Lark%20Myca%20Roux.md).

### Alistrassa's cantrips

**Identify** — divination. Points the caster at the knowledge she needs: in a library, the shelf; in a room, the ledger under the dead man's quill. One question per cast.
**Comprehension** — divination. Consume a text faster than reading it. Played as interrogating the GM about what it says, within reason. Costs her like any cast ([Karmic Wasting](#karmic-wasting)).

## First-Level Spells and Beyond

### Fungal Shield

- **Source**: Gifted.
- **School**: Abjuration and Evocation. Both are Neutral for Gifted, so nothing mechanical rides on the call. Not a new "hybrid" category — the ruling is just to list both schools a spell touches rather than invent one.
- **Components**: Verbal or Somatic — either alone is enough. No material component, so the spell does not force a Planned Action. A bound caster (arms restrained, mouth free) casts through Verbal; a gagged caster (mouth restrained, arms free) casts through Somatic. Bound and gagged together, they cannot cast it.
- **Action**: Castable as an action **or** as a reaction. Cast as an action, it cannot also be cast as a reaction that turn; other reactions the caster has are unaffected.
- **Range**: Two zones. See [Ranged Combat](../Mechanics/Questing%20Time!%20Mechanics.md#ranged-combat).
- **Effect**: A large mushroom springs into existence and takes the blow. Incoming damage is reduced by one step on the Health track. Cast it on the caster or on one ally.
- **Upcast**: Advances the effect, not the dice pool. Either more reactions per round, or stronger protection.

The shield blocks physical attacks and Evocation only. It does nothing against the other five schools. The anti-magic version — nature resisting a spell as it moves, a caster's hands caught in tendrils — is held back on purpose, as a separate and later spell.

The spell is modeled on *Shield* from Dungeons & Dragons. That was a spec reference, not a name.

Holder: [Iter Bravus](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md).

### Greater Invisibility

- **Source**: Derived. **School**: Abjuration.
- **Effect**: The target does **not** vanish — it stays fully visible — but it generates **greatly reduced sound**: footfalls, breath, a drawn blade, all near-silent. Objects the target carries are covered by the effect. Detection then leans on hearing far less and on sight far more.
- **Does not end on the target attacking or casting** — unlike ordinary invisibility, it holds through aggressive action.
- **Wording is first-pass**, dictated at the table in Session Two; to be tightened — open at Rules.

Demonstrated by: [Snarfa](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#snarfa) (cast on Bob for the depot climb).
