Default stat blocks for [Questing Time!](../Lore/Questing%20Time!%20A%20Primer.md). Rules: [Questing Time! Mechanics](../Mechanics/Questing%20Time!%20Mechanics.md). Abilities: [Abilities](../Mechanics/Abilities.md).

Each character holds one D20, one D12, one D10, one D8, one D6, and one D4. Assign one die to each stat. Every block below is a permutation of that set.

These are the default blocks for **any** character of a given race — player character or NPC alike. There is no separate "playable race" restriction. Adjust individual NPCs from their racial default as the story calls for it, the same way a player would advance their own character (see [Advancement](../Mechanics/Questing%20Time!%20Mechanics.md#advancement)).

# Races

## Skews

A block is **race + skew**: the race supplies six dice, the skew says how they are ordered. This is the generator for most named characters, and it is why a new NPC costs a line rather than a paragraph.

**Caster skews** are the worked family. A caster's block is the race's block with a skew set by the practice. Take the race's six dice and sort them. The practice's pair takes the top two. Of the two pairs left, the one the race already favours takes the middle two and the other takes the bottom two. Within a pair the larger die goes to the stat the race favours; a player may swap.

| Practice | High pair |
| --- | --- |
| **Derived** — wizards, the College's | Intelligence, Wisdom |
| **Innate** — sorcerers | Prowess, Constitution |
| **Gifted** — druids, clerics, warlocks | Charisma, Instinct |

Racial abilities are unchanged by the skew. Each race below lists its three skewed blocks. A player character keeps the dice on their sheet.

**Other skews follow the same shape** — name the pair that goes on top and the rest falls out. The **prowess skew** is in play already: Train pirate (crew of three) is the Munchkin base with Prowess and Instinct swapped, which is what makes a gun crew dangerous without making any of them a duellist. A **background** skew (an aristocrat, say, with Charisma and Instinct on top) is a second such family, alongside the caster family above

## Drau

**Illuminated — the Drau are dark elves**. A subrace of Elf: a Drau *is* an Elf, on the [Karmic Order](../Lore/Magic%20System.md#the-karmic-order), and reincarnates as an Elf. This is why a Drau relates to Innate magic casually — see [Iter Bravus](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md)'s notes.

D20 Instinct; D12 Wisdom; D10 Intelligence; D8 Charisma; D6 Constitution; D4 Prowess.

Abilities: [Night Vision](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#night-vision).

Night vision holds for every Drau, with no exception — cave-dwelling and city-dwelling alike. A Drau sees perfectly in the dark. In bright light, a Drau rolls Constitution before any check that depends on perceiving the environment, and fails that roll into disadvantage on the check. [Sunglasses](../Mechanics/Items%20and%20Equipment.md#sunglasses) cancel the penalty. [Night Vision](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#night-vision) holds the full mechanic.

City Drau also hold [Smoothtalker](#smoothtalker). That is a city-dweller trait, not a racial one — see [City Dwellers](#city-dwellers).

## Dwarves

The City's industrial folk: train-depot logistics, and what fine industry happens locally — jewelry, weapons, armor, useful artifice. Resides in: [New Squishnesta](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md). The setting's industrial race; they hold [the Mechanics Guild](../Lore/Factions.md#the-mechanics-guild).

D20 Constitution; D12 Prowess; D10 Wisdom; D8 Intelligence; D6 Charisma; D4 Instinct.

High constitution, relatively high prowess and wisdom, and intelligence a little below both — a hard-working, level-headed, unbookish people.

**Illuminated** — Dwarves are on the [Karmic Order](../Lore/Magic%20System.md#the-karmic-order). Their **pairing axis** (Lawful/Chaotic like Elves, Good/Evil like Fey, or their own) is open.

## Half-Giants

A half-race per [Half-Races](#half-races) — no fixed formula, built by merging the two parent races' dice case by case. [Pudge](../Player%20Sheets/Pudge.md) is the first. The second parent race is presumed **Human** (unconfirmed). Now that Giants have a base block (above), the formal Half-Giant default can be merged from Giant + Human; until that's done, Pudge's own dice stand as his personal build, which the half-race rule allows.

**Pudge's build:** D20 Constitution; D12 Instinct; D10 Prowess; D8 Wisdom; D6 Charisma; D4 Intelligence. (Prowess and Instinct are swapped versus the Giant default — a personal deviation, allowed.)

**Racial ability: [Beefy](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#beefy)** — from the Giant side, not Pudge's alone. [Magical Dampening](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#magical-dampening) is College-granted, not racial.

# City Dwellers

Some traits key to where a character was raised, not to their race. Any race can hold them.

## Smoothtalker

[Smoothtalker](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#smoothtalker) is a city-dweller trait. It is not a racial ability, and it is not tied to the Drau.

Holders:
- Some characters of [New Squishnesta](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md), case by case.
- **The Drau ancestral cave city's metropolitan center** — confirmed. It's a genuine metropolis, so its Drau count as city-dwellers the same as any other. See [The UKaves](../Lore/Places/The%20UKaves.md).

It does **not** hold for cave-dwelling Drau scattered through the periphery caves — the smaller settlements ringing the ancestral city and elsewhere. They aren't metropolitan, so they don't qualify. This is the same ruling as above, not a separate exception.

# Half-Races

No fixed formula. Build a new default block for the specific mix by merging the two parent races' dice, case by case, as a general principle. A half-race build may also break the one-die-per-stat rule: the racial mixing can eliminate a parent's D20 or D4 outright, or double down and accentuate it further, at the GM's discretion.
