Default stat blocks for [Questing Time!](../Lore/Questing%20Time!%20A%20Primer.md). Rules: [Questing Time! Mechanics](../Mechanics/Questing%20Time!%20Mechanics.md). Abilities: [Abilities](../Mechanics/Abilities.md).

Each character holds one D20, one D12, one D10, one D8, one D6, and one D4. Assign one die to each stat. Every block below is a permutation of that set.

These are the default blocks for **any** character of a given race — player character or NPC alike. There is no separate "playable race" restriction;. Adjust individual NPCs from their racial default as the story calls for it, the same way a player would advance their own character (see [Advancement](../Mechanics/Questing%20Time!%20Mechanics.md#advancement)).

# Races

## Caster skews

A caster's block is the race's block with a skew set by the practice. Take the race's six dice and sort them. The practice's pair takes the top two. Of the two pairs left, the one the race already favours takes the middle two and the other takes the bottom two. Within a pair the larger die goes to the stat the race favours; a player may swap.

| Practice | High pair |
| --- | --- |
| **Derived** — wizards, the College's | Intelligence, Wisdom |
| **Innate** — sorcerers | Prowess, Constitution |
| **Gifted** — druids, clerics, warlocks | Charisma, Instinct |

Racial abilities are unchanged by the skew. Each race below lists its three skewed blocks. A player character keeps the dice on their sheet.

## Drau

**Illuminated — the Drau are dark elves**. A subrace of Elf: a Drau *is* an Elf, on the [Karmic Order](../Lore/Magic%20System.md#the-karmic-order), and reincarnates as an Elf. This is why a Drau relates to Innate magic casually — see [Iter Bravus](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md)'s notes.

D20 Instinct; D12 Wisdom; D10 Intelligence; D8 Charisma; D6 Constitution; D4 Prowess.

Abilities: [Night Vision](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#night-vision).

Night vision holds for every Drau, with no exception — cave-dwelling and city-dwelling alike. A Drau sees perfectly in the dark. In bright light, a Drau rolls Constitution before any check that depends on perceiving the environment, and fails that roll into disadvantage on the check. [Sunglasses](../Mechanics/Items%20and%20Equipment.md#sunglasses) cancel the penalty. [Night Vision](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#night-vision) holds the full mechanic.

City Drau also hold [Smoothtalker](#smoothtalker). That is a city-dweller trait, not a racial one — see [City Dwellers](#city-dwellers).

## Dwarves

The City's industrial folk: train-depot logistics, and what fine industry happens locally — jewelry, weapons, armor, useful artifice. Resides in: [New Squishnesta](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md). The setting's industrial race; they hold [the Mechanics Guild](../Lore/Factions.md#the-mechanics-guild).

D20 Constitution; D12 Prowess; D10 Intelligence; D8 Wisdom; D6 Charisma; D4 Instinct.

**Illuminated** — Dwarves are on the [Karmic Order](../Lore/Magic%20System.md#the-karmic-order),. Their **pairing axis** (Lawful/Chaotic like Elves, Good/Evil like Fey, or their own) is open.

## Half-Giants

A half-race per [Half-Races](#half-races) — no fixed formula, built by merging the two parent races' dice case by case. [Pudge](../Player%20Sheets/Pudge.md) is the first. The second parent race is presumed **Human** (unconfirmed). Now that Giants have a base block (above), the formal Half-Giant default can be merged from Giant + Human; until that's done, Pudge's own dice stand as his personal build, which the half-race rule allows.

**Pudge's build:** D20 Constitution; D12 Instinct; D10 Prowess; D8 Wisdom; D6 Charisma; D4 Intelligence. (Prowess and Instinct are swapped versus the Giant default — a personal deviation, allowed.)

**Racial ability: [Beefy](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#beefy)** — from the Giant side, per the 2026-08-29 ruling; no longer just Pudge's personal ability. [Magical Dampening](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#magical-dampening) is College-granted, not racial.

# City Dwellers

Some traits key to where a character was raised, not to their race. Any race can hold them.

## Smoothtalker

[Smoothtalker](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#smoothtalker) is a city-dweller trait. It is not a racial ability, and it is not tied to the Drau.

Holders:
- Some characters of [New Squishnesta](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md), case by case.
- **The Drau ancestral cave city's metropolitan center** — confirmed. It's a genuine metropolis, so its Drau count as city-dwellers the same as any other. See [The UKaves](../Lore/Questing%20Time!%20Geography.md#the-ukaves).

It does **not** hold for cave-dwelling Drau scattered through the periphery caves — the smaller settlements ringing the ancestral city and elsewhere. They aren't metropolitan, so they don't qualify. This is the same ruling as above, not a separate exception.

# Half-Races

No fixed formula. Build a new default block for the specific mix by merging the two parent races' dice, case by case, as a general principle. A half-race build may also break the one-die-per-stat rule: the racial mixing can eliminate a parent's D20 or D4 outright, or double down and accentuate it further, at the GM's discretion.

## Finnegan Bramblebush

@FINN Finnegan Bramblebush — Pro D4 · Con D10 · Int D6 · Wis D8 · Cha D20 · Ins D12 — [Open Book](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#open-book); Sweet Blood at half effect

Half Munchkin, half human. Run on the base Munchkin block, unmodified; his permanent half-race build is still owed under [Half-Races](#half-races). Open Book fits him badly on purpose: he has no charges, he reads as honest because he believes himself. Works out of [the Tavern](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md#key-locations) and handed out the Session Zero fetch-quest contract. GM-named, 2026-08-16. Resides in: [New Squishnesta](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md).

A heel to all five families and therefore their common go-between; knows the heads of all five, none of whom like him. Runs candy for the College with Bill the Butcher, for the promise of a homestead that never existed. Blabbers, inconsistently and mostly wrongly — the wrong parts are the parts he is most confident about. **Half-brother to [Bramblebeard](#bramblebeard-thramplesnatch)**, one Munchkin parent of the Bramble line; the street mixes the two up. If pressed on who wanted him dead he has two honest wrong answers — Bill's people, or the Don — and does not know the true one.

**Session One (2026-09-02):** sold to the Don by the party (now his Enemies), interrogated under greater truth serum, then taken from the Don's back room. Last seen: Major damage, in their custody. A reverse Gilear: everyone wants him dead and he will not die.

Voice: frightened blabbermouth, brightening the moment anyone is kind to him.

## Don Aldo Zuccaro

@DON Don Aldo Zuccaro — Pro D4 · Con D10 · Int D6 · Wis D8 · Cha D20 · Ins D12 — [Well Connected](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#well-connected); [Open Book](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#open-book)
@DON at the table (negotiation) — Pro D4 · Con D10 · Int D6 · Wis D20 · Cha D12 · Ins D8

Munchkin. Head of the Zuccaro family — La Familia Munquiana — the old blood of [the Red Line](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md#key-locations); see [The Don](../Lore/Factions.md#the-don). White suit, black collar, bow tie, a sugar-plum cigar, a cat, cognac older than most of his guests. Open Book on a man this old is the joke: he answers honestly and it tells you nothing. Resides in: [New Squishnesta](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md).

Voice: old-country patriarch, slow and warm; every sentence is a favour being done.

## Dean Alistair

@ALISTAIR Dean Alistair — Pro D10 · Con D8 · Int D12 · Wis D20 · Cha D4 · Ins D6 — [Wizardry](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#wizardry) (Restoration); [Ancient Instinct](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#ancient-instinct)

High Elf, on the [High Elves](#high-elves) spread with the **Wizard skew** ([Caster skews](#caster-skews)). Dean of **Restoration** at [The College of Mages](../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md): old, long white robe, long white beard, the hat. His given name is "Dean" — "Dean Dean," to the table's lasting amusement. Reads his books up the spire and is not doing much else. Blunt about death and sorry about it; funds a karmic-restoration desk nobody will sit at and hired [Alistrassa](../Player%20Sheets/Alistrassa.md) to it the day she arrived, folio in hand. **Inscrutable**: a read on him at D10 tells you he seems kind, and nothing else. Resides in: [New Squishnesta](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md) (the College).

**Session Two (2026-09-09):** brought Alistrassa the rumour of "adventurers carrying a College rune" and nudged her hard to look into it — read as under-informed and pushing research onto her, not as a man who knew what he was sending her toward.

Voice: mild, grandfatherly, apologises for the prognosis while giving it.

## Snarfa

@SNARFA Snarfa — Pro D6 · Con D8 · Int D20 · Wis D10 · Cha D4 · Ins D12 — **Greater** — [Wizardry](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#wizardry) (Abjuration); [Well Connected](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#well-connected)

Reads as a human on the Derived skew with Instinct and Charisma raised, and the dice are proposed until a Celestial block exists. **The super senior**: fifty years a student, never graduated, better than some of the professors and paid to teach, sits the Abjuration clerk's desk in flip-flops, a fleece and board shorts, frequently high. Smoke-ring runes off a joint; a sudoku whose numbers get up and dance; hacky sack on the quad. Learned glassblowing from Ellis once. Knows the sun god personally. Wards a rig with a comically small spray can: "Snarfa was here." One of the -artha family. **Aligned with Alistrassa**. Resides in: [New Squishnesta](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md) (the College).

**Session Two (2026-09-09) — allied with the party, intermittently.** Answered Alistrassa's chit at once and came to the depot on a longboard. At the table he held the moon still for ~6 hours so a cover "party" cost the party no time; opened a hole that dropped Bartholomew into another plane and that the half-giants reverse-stacked crates through; made Bob near-silent for the climb ([Greater Invisibility](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#greater-invisibility)); painted an augury rune on a wall that showed the whole railway on a half-folio; and lifted five loaded crates one-handed. He drew the killing rune out of the velvet pouch before it reached Alistrassa. **~5 workings left of the evening; unhurt.** Now Neutral with the party (Aligned with Alistrassa), **+1 stage due after the depot fight**. An **ongoing ally, but not always available — and not always on this plane.**

Voice: surfer, unhurried, "hell yeah"; helps by knowing someone, never by working.
