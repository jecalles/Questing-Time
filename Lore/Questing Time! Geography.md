Squish Nesta is the world. Nobody knows what happened to Old Squishnesta. Some say it never existed.

# New Squishnesta

Colloquially "the City." A techno-magical metropolis of fifty to a hundred thousand people, maybe four generations old, with no walls: it fades outward, less and less dense, until it is all pastoral. It sits inland, on a river with a waterfall, with [the Castle](../Lore/Places/The%20Castle.md) on a cliff at its heart and [the College of Mages](../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md) abutting it. Freight trains float in on abjurative runes with [the Shire](../Lore/Places/Squishnestashire.md)'s produce. It smells faintly of bubblegum.

Its districts: the Castle and the College; the General Market, the Commons and [the Tavern](../Lore/Places/The%20Tavern.md) in a cluster near the centre; the Whitecourse, where the aristocracy lives; a light industrial quarter; the Train Station where the lines converge; and **[the Red Line](../Lore/Places/The%20Red%20Line.md)**, the Munchkin district, ringed by a band of red brick eight or ten bricks deep, where the candy is a drug and five families run the trade. The Red Line has its own tavern, a well, an anarchist's library, and, at its edge opposite the Castle, a rusted and half-disused **train depot**. The sewers run into the caves below. Full entries: [Key Locations](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md#key-locations).

# Squishnestashire

The farm country round [the City](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md), out to one northern coast. See [Squishnestashire](../Lore/Places/Squishnestashire.md).

## The River Squish

The river that runs through [the City](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md) and glistens. See [The River Squish](../Lore/Places/The%20River%20Squish.md).

## Hamlet

The only named village in [the Shire](../Lore/Places/Squishnestashire.md), where the party's first job went. See [Hamlet](../Lore/Places/Hamlet.md).

## Trains

The abjurative lines run radially out from the City, with little or no connection between villages. That is a design, not an accident: it keeps [the Shire](../Lore/Places/Squishnestashire.md)'s villages from organising against the City, and it makes the region's politics extractive. Mechanism and economics: [The Trains](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md#the-trains).

## Questing Boards

Most small towns hold one; a city may hold several. Anyone can pin a flyer; no introduction is needed between poster and party. [The City](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md)'s is in the town square, and [the Tavern](../Lore/Places/The%20Tavern.md) is where you meet whoever took the job.

# The Sharp Forest

At [the Shire](../Lore/Places/Squishnestashire.md)'s edge: dense forest and bog, sparsely peopled at its perimeter and not at all deep in, where huge silicon monoliths innervated by trees and mycelium hum and glow. The name comes from the bite of the vegetation, not the silicon. Home to the Druids of the Forest, the Forest Fae of Razor's Edge, and the Anachrons — silicon automata that guard what they were built to guard. The Unseelie Fey are pushing in from a domain of their own elsewhere. [Lark](../Player%20Sheets/Lark%20Myca%20Roux.md) is from here; so, in a sense she does not fully understand, is her magic. Full entry: [Sharp Forest](../Lore/Sharp%20Forest.md).

Same continent as [the City](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md), deeper in. If the City is western Europe running out to its coast, the Forest is the steppe gone to forest: not arid but sunken and green, more humid the further in, with deep rivers and tributaries running every way.

# The UKaves

The cave system beneath everything, which [Iter](../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md) and Bob walked out of on the first day. See [The UKaves](../Lore/Places/The%20UKaves.md).

# Between the regions

- **City to Shire:** the roads, and the freight lines. [Hamlet](../Lore/Places/Hamlet.md) is a long day's walk.
- **City to Forest:** the Forest begins where [the Shire](../Lore/Places/Squishnestashire.md)'s farms give out; [Lark](../Player%20Sheets/Lark%20Myca%20Roux.md) floated out of it onto the Shire's edge.
- **Surface to UKaves:** the sewers, the cave mouth by [the Castle](../Lore/Places/The%20Castle.md) cliff, and whatever the Forest's bogs hide.
- **Anywhere to anywhere, for mages:** fixed portal sites, controlled and rent-extracted by [the College](../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md), reserved in practice for the aristocracy.
