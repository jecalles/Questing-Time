# The Lay of the Land

Four regions, and the ways between them. Read this first; each region has its own note.

| Region | What it is | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **New Squishnesta** | the City: techno-magical, landlocked, young, walled by nothing | [New Squishnesta](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md) |
| **Squishnestashire** | the farm country around it, out to one northern coast | below |
| **The Sharp Forest** | dense forest and bog at the Shire's edge, older than the City | Sharp Forest |
| **The UKaves** | the cave system under all of it, and the way down | below |

Squish Nesta is the world. Nobody knows what happened to Old Squishnesta. Some say it never existed.

# New Squishnesta

Colloquially "the City." A techno-magical metropolis of fifty to a hundred thousand people, maybe four generations old, with no walls: it fades outward, less and less dense, until it is all pastoral. It sits inland, on a river with a waterfall, with the Castle on a cliff at its heart and the College of Mages abutting it. Freight trains float in on abjurative runes with the Shire's produce. It smells faintly of bubblegum.

Its districts: the Castle and the College; the General Market, the Commons and the Tavern in a cluster near the centre; the Whitecourse, where the aristocracy lives; a light industrial quarter; the Train Station where the lines converge; and **the Red Line**, the Munchkin district, ringed by a band of red brick eight or ten bricks deep, where the candy is a drug and five families run the trade. The Red Line has its own tavern, a well, an anarchist's library, and, at its edge opposite the Castle, a rusted and half-disused **train depot**. The sewers run into the caves below. Full entries: [Key Locations](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md#key-locations).

# Squishnestashire

The Shire: the agricultural region around the City, roughly three hundred thousand people across farms, orchards and villages, most of it monocropping under hard conditions. Politically fragmented on purpose — see the trains, below — and not all of it loyal to New Squishnesta; some Shire polities feel more allegiance to other regional towns. Its people are humans and Munchkins, farmers who want to be left alone ([The Homesteaders](../Lore/Factions.md#the-homesteaders)).

## The Coast

The Shire has one coast, to the north, with a small seafaring trade of fishing and whaling.

## The River Squish

A river runs through the region and the City, with a waterfall. It glistens; there is something like innate magic in the water itself, a deliberate opposite to the College's derived-magic industry.

Its older name is **Brightwater**, which is what the glisten was named for. It is the **River Squish** now, and has been since the Munchkins' land became somebody else's city — the same renaming habit that turned Munchkinland into New Squishnesta. Old maps and old people still say Brightwater.

## Hamlet

The only named village, about a thousand people, deep in the Shire — roughly halfway in, a long walk from the City. Big enough for a general store, a tavern and some communal life. The party's first job took them there; the tavern floor has not recovered.

## Trains

The abjurative lines run radially out from the City, with little or no connection between villages. That is a design, not an accident: it keeps the Shire's villages from organising against the City, and it makes the region's politics extractive. Mechanism and economics: [The Trains](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md#the-trains).

## Questing Boards

Most small towns hold one; a city may hold several. Anyone can pin a flyer; no introduction is needed between poster and party. The City's is in the town square, and the Tavern is where you meet whoever took the job.

# The Sharp Forest

At the Shire's edge: dense forest and bog, sparsely peopled at its perimeter and not at all deep in, where huge silicon monoliths innervated by trees and mycelium hum and glow. The name comes from the bite of the vegetation, not the silicon. Home to the Druids of the Forest, the Forest Fae of Razor's Edge, and the Anachrons — silicon automata that guard what they were built to guard. The Unseelie Fey are pushing in from a domain of their own elsewhere. Lark is from here; so, in a sense she does not fully understand, is her magic. Full entry: Sharp Forest.

# The UKaves

The cave system beneath everything. Its tunnels, excavated over millennia, are dense and interconnected near the surface; deeper in they trend toward delves. The City's sewers open into it, and a cave mouth sits near the Castle's cliff. Iter and Bob walked out of it into New Squishnesta on the first day of the campaign — Iter leading, Bob blind — and when a Munchkin asked where they were from, Iter said "the UK," and the name stuck.

What people in the City know is narrow: there are caves under New Squishnesta, and they go deep. Where they go is unclear, and the College of Mages keeps archaeologists and geologists on the question without closing it.

Known places in the UKaves:

- **The Drau ancestral cave city** — an underground metropolis and the Drau homeland, deep in the system: a false night sky thrown across the cavern ceiling, jazz-age money and manners, airships crossing beneath the rock, thinning out through the adjoining caves from metropolitan to rural. It has no name yet. Iter was born into its ruling class and left it. See [City Drau](../Lore/Factions.md#city-drau).
- **The delves, and the Heart** — the deep places the tunnels trend toward. Bob climbed out of the Heart.

# Between the regions

- **City to Shire:** the roads, and the freight lines. Hamlet is a long day's walk.
- **City to Forest:** the Forest begins where the Shire's farms give out; Lark floated out of it onto the Shire's edge.
- **Surface to UKaves:** the sewers, the cave mouth by the Castle cliff, and whatever the Forest's bogs hide.
- **Anywhere to anywhere, for mages:** fixed portal sites, controlled and rent-extracted by the College, reserved in practice for the aristocracy.
