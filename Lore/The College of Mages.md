# The College of Mages

The seat of Derived magic in [New Squishnesta](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md), and the political power that sits beside the Castle — the faction entry is [The College of Mages](../Lore/Factions.md#the-college-of-mages); this note is the building and the people in it. "The Harvard of the wizarding world," and, on the inside, a party school that runs on its network.

# The Hexagon

Six schools, a spire each, small rooms off every spire, and one shared quad in the middle. The building abuts [the Castle](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md#key-locations). Perhaps thirty academics, plus students, clerks, and one super senior. Books on every shelf of every hall: the whole place is the library, and the schools are its wings. Stairs in every spire, worn wood and good carpet; floating stones for the trafficked stairwells, with a queue; and the creatures who can fly simply do.

# The Two Councils

**The Academic Council.** Six deans, one per school, and there is nothing above them. Vacancies are filled by election among the remaining members. They hold a **courtesy seat in government** for ruling on magical affairs, which rarely comes up, and otherwise tend to the College's administration. **Entirely elven**, with one exception: [Victoria Ashburne](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#victoria-ashburne), Dean of Evocation, a human sorcerer. There are not many evocation wizards willing to *study* — it is a vibe-based practice — so the chair tends to go to whichever sorcerer is too powerful to refuse.

**The Bureaucratic Council.** Procurement, Ways and Means, Groundskeeping, and more as they are needed. Nominally subservient to the Academic Council, whose decisions the College's magical charter would enforce. In practice wizards are airheads with no attention for the day-to-day, so the Bureaucratic Council runs it — and **most of the actual worldly power lies here.** Largely human.

# How the College Works

**Chits.** Nobody browses. You write a small chit — one inkpot, one book, a number — and the book comes to you. The chit is an IOU of magical expression; every wizard is paying into a pot of books-coming-to-you and books-going-away, and the same paper, scaled up, runs the College's real business. A quarto carries a serious working; a full folio, countersigned by wizards, has ceded countries. You will get a chit back for what you borrow, and nobody enforces the return so much as remembers it. See [Ways-and-Means chits](../Mechanics/Items%20and%20Equipment.md#ways-and-means-chits).

**Indexing is social.** The library has no catalogue a stranger can read. Clerks know where things are; the only index you own is the one you write yourself, as an inscription of your own. Ask the clerk of the school whose subject you want.

**Who studies here.** Predominantly elves, with a number of humans; the upper ranks elven, the scholarship largely human (see [The College of Mages](../Lore/Factions.md#the-college-of-mages)). For an elf, magical study is writing a history of having been magical. For a human, it is discovery, and the human scholars fill the shelves.

**The magic is in the ink.** Runes are drawn with an ink that shimmers as it goes down; the caster spends nothing. What the student learns is the calligraphy of it — "the flick" — because the shape and the motion are the spell. See [Runes](../Lore/Magic%20System.md#runes).

**Karmic medicine.** The Restoration clinic sees bodies; it does not treat karma. "We practise none of it. We're humans, you see." A karmic-restoration desk exists, funded and unstaffed, because the only people who care about karmic disease are the people currently dying of it, and the last holder died at the desk. As of 2026-09-06 the desk is [Alistrassa](../Player%20Sheets/Alistrassa.md)'s.

# Places Inside

- **The Restoration clinic.** Ground floor of the Restoration spire. Tim Aboul, and a hand-wave for bruises.
- **The dean's study, Restoration.** Up the spire; a small vestibule with a fairy at a desk; the dean reads.
- **The dead wizard's library.** Off the dean's study: a messy room, a ledger in blue ink with a splatter where the pen stopped, twenty-three case files, all deceased.
- **The Abjuration writing room.** Ink, quills, crumpled paper, the intro shelf, and whoever is stuck on their homework.
- **The amphitheatre.** Charred stone, fire wizards letting off steam, one of them in a bucket.
- **Transmutation's labs and tool shop.** Where a thing gets made once someone has decided what it is.
- **The quad.** Hacky sack, and the party when there is one.

# Outside the Walls

**Ellis's shop.** A Munchkin-scale spectacles shop on the south end of town by [the Red Line](../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md#key-locations). Ellis grinds bifocals for Munchkins; he taught Snarfa glassblowing once; he will make you lenses in a few weeks. A local library sits beside the industrial part of town where the glassworkers are.
