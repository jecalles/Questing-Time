# Ellis's Shop

**Ellis's shop.** A Munchkin-scale spectacles shop on the south end of town, by [the Red Line](../../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md#key-locations). [Ellis](../../NPC%20Sheets/Ellis.md) grinds bifocals for Munchkins. He taught [Snarfa](../../NPC%20Sheets/Snarfa.md) glassblowing once. He will make you lenses in a few weeks. A local library sits beside the industrial part of town, where the glassworkers are.
