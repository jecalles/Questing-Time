# The River Squish

A river runs through the region and [the City](../../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md), with a waterfall. It glistens; there is something like innate magic in the water itself, a deliberate opposite to [the College](../../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md)'s derived-magic industry.

Its older name is **Brightwater**, which is what the glisten was named for. It is the **River Squish** now, and has been since the Munchkins' land became somebody else's city — the same renaming habit that turned Munchkinland into New Squishnesta. Old maps and old people still say Brightwater.
