# The UKaves

The cave system beneath everything. Its tunnels, excavated over millennia, are dense and interconnected near the surface; deeper in they trend toward delves. [The City](../../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md)'s sewers open into it, and a cave mouth sits near [the Castle](../../Lore/Places/The%20Castle.md)'s cliff. [Iter](../../Player%20Sheets/Iter%20Bravus.md) and Bob walked out of it into New Squishnesta on the first day of the campaign — Iter leading, Bob blind — and when a Munchkin asked where they were from, Iter said "the UK," and the name stuck.

What people in the City know is narrow: there are caves under New Squishnesta, and they go deep. Where they go is unclear, and [the College of Mages](../../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md) keeps archaeologists and geologists on the question without closing it.

Known places in the UKaves:

- **The Drau ancestral cave city** — an underground metropolis and the Drau homeland, deep in the system, thinning out through the adjoining caves from metropolitan to rural. Its name, together with its counterpart above, is **the Lunderground** — two cities, not one: the **Bottom End** (Drau, floor, Victorian) and the **Top End** (Fey, ceiling, jazz-age), meeting at the skyline.
- **The Top End** — the Lunderground's other half, hanging from the stalactites above the Bottom End: Fey and other spirits, feet on the ceiling, jazz-age money and manners, airships crossing beneath the rock. New, and largely unbuilt.
- **The delves, and the Heart** — the deep places the tunnels trend toward. Bob climbed out of the Heart.
