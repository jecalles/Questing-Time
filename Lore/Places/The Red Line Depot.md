# The Red Line Depot

**The Red Line Depot**: a rusted, half-disused rail depot at the edge of [the Red Line](../../Lore/Places/The%20Red%20Line.md) opposite [the Castle](../../Lore/Places/The%20Castle.md), where the [Bootleggers](../../Lore/Factions.md#the-bootleggers) cut cars off [the College](../../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md)'s trains. Run by its own logistics, not by the College, which is a contractor here and nothing more. Foreman: Phineas Smithson; [Bartholomew Groundshaker](../../Player%20Materials/People%20You've%20Met.md#bartholomew-groundshaker) and a handful of other dwarves work it. The one fence in the Red Line rings it — about ten feet of wood, not sturdy but loud to breach. The warehouse walls are holed.
