# Squishnestashire

The Shire: the agricultural region around [the City](../../Lore/New%20Squishnesta.md), roughly three hundred thousand people across farms, orchards and villages, most of it monocropping under hard conditions. Politically fragmented on purpose — see the trains, below — and not all of it loyal to New Squishnesta; some Shire polities feel more allegiance to other regional towns. Its people are humans and Munchkins, farmers who want to be left alone ([The Homesteaders](../../Lore/Factions.md#the-homesteaders)).
