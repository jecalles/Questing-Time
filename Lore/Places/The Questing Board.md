# The Questing Board

**The Questing Board**: a central town-square board for posting jobs to adventurers. See [Questing Boards](../../Lore/Questing%20Time!%20Geography.md#questing-boards). [The Tavern](../../Lore/Places/The%20Tavern.md) is the typical place to meet and accept a posted quest in person, once taken.
