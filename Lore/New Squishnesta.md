# Overview
Colloquially "The City", a techno-magical metropolis. Fed by abjurative floating traincars filled with the local produce from the surrounding Squishnestashire, a sparse agrarian community. Economy is mostly agricultural and magical services to other vassal realms. Smells faintly of bubblegum.

Nobody knows what happened to "Old Squishnesta". Some say it never existed. 

The City is young — maybe four generations old. It has no walls: it just fades outward, less and less dense, until it's all pastoral. Population 50,000–100,000, GM leaning 50,000 (Squishnestashire as a whole holds ~300,000 — see [Questing Time! Geography](../Lore/Questing%20Time!%20Geography.md)). On its surface it looks like a near-utopia that governs itself without blood — because the society genuinely works for the people in power. Criminal behavior rarely goes through any legal system: nobody sane crosses the College of Mages, and if a minor adept rips you to shreds for trying, that's held to be your own fault. The dynamic isn't tyranny — it's a human not caring what the ant colony next door does. If a prison exists at all, it's the rinky-dink kind you'd expect of a small hamlet (tentative, GM).

# The Trains

The traincars don't actually abnegate gravity — that's a common in-world misunderstanding. Abjurative runes on the underside of the cars and on the tracks repel each other, like positive and negative charges; a gentle push of evocation magic moves the cars along. The runes are easy to maintain in place, but sending a field technician out to fix or lay new ones is expensive — rune maintenance is presumed to be a large portion of the economy. The trains are set up for agriculture and goods, not people: the College of Mages benefits most from them politically, and mages can simply teleport via Derived conjuration. Fixed portal sites likely exist, controlled and rent-extracted (tentative, GM). Line layout and its politics: [Trains](../Lore/Questing%20Time!%20Geography.md#trains).

The Shire's agricultural output is mostly monocropping under somewhat austere practices — the surrounding environment pushed hard to subsist — plus a small seafaring trade of minor fishing and whaling off the northern coast.

# Key Locations

**The Tavern**: formerly the Broken Drum, then the Mended Drum, then the Battered Drum, and finally just the Tavern after they gave up on fixing it. In Session Zero the GM used **"the Battered Drum"** as the current formal name. Sits close to the General Market and the Commons, not far from the Red Line. Owned and run by [Bob, going by "Robert"](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#bob-name-tbd). This is also where the Session Zero fetch-quest flyer is posted and where [Finnegan Bramblebush](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#finnegan-bramblebush) works, handing the contract to whoever answers it. Not to be confused with Hamlet's tavern in Squishnestashire, a separate, established location — see [Hamlet](../Lore/Questing%20Time!%20Geography.md#hamlet).

**The Castle**: the seat of culture and power. Aristocratic, gaudy, insular. 

**The General Store**: genre-aware shop and shopkeep. A profitable front for the Back Room, the gateway to the blackmarket of the City. The shopkeep is cheerful, helpful, and unassuming.

**The Well** - a mysterious and magical well, inside [the Red Line](#key-locations). A somewhat well-known landmark within the district; its secrets are not. Congregating spot for feyfolk and their friends with secrets only visible to them.

**The Questing Board**: a central town-square board for posting jobs to adventurers. See [Questing Boards](../Lore/Questing%20Time!%20Geography.md#questing-boards). The Tavern is the typical place to meet and accept a posted quest in person, once taken.

**The Red Line**: the Munchkin district, ringed by a road paved in red brick. A redlined ghetto in function if not in name — the name is a deliberate nod to historical redlining, crossed with the yellow brick road. The population is predominantly Munchkins, plus lesser and minor fey. Publicly, it's known only as a seedy district with illicit business behind its doors — the families themselves are not common knowledge. Densely populated and rough: the first thing that hits you is the smell of putrefying candy and sugar rotting in the streets, and the drug trade is out in the open — Munchkins mainlining lit "taffy" on the sidewalk. Official cartographers don't map it in any detail; to the College's mapmakers it's just a ghetto of no interest. It holds a tavern-equivalent of its own, and an enterprising group of Munchkins runs an **anarchist's library** there, doing small amounts of community organizing — not yet enough to be a threatening faction, but one the players can interact with. See [The Five Families](../Lore/Factions.md#the-five-families) and [Substances](../Mechanics/Items%20and%20Equipment.md#substances).

**Walking the Red Line.** The district has **no hard walls** — the red brick is a road, not a barrier, and anyone may walk it. From the Don's home to Bill's block is a plain walk through the ghetto, the scenery shifting, corner kids somewhere on the way; there are only streets in between. Bill's shop, **Bambi the Baker's** bakery next door and **Kathy the Candlestick Maker** are one block. The depot sits at the far edge, opposite the Castle; Millbank Manor at the inner one. The corner kids are diffuse and sprawl over every street; Ruby is not usually among them, being posted somewhere secluded, doing the numbers.

**The Train Station**: the central hub and depot where the abjurative lines converge. Goods and produce in, refined imports through. See [The Trains](#the-trains).

**The Red Line Depot**: a rusted, half-disused rail depot at the edge of the Red Line opposite the Castle, where the [Bootleggers](../Lore/Factions.md#the-bootleggers) cut cars off the College's trains. Run by its own logistics, not by the College, which is a contractor here and nothing more. Foreman: [Phineas Smithson](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#phineas-smithson); [Bartholomew Groundshaker](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#bartholomew-groundshaker) and a handful of other dwarves work it. The one fence in the Red Line rings it — about ten feet of wood, not sturdy but loud to breach. The warehouse walls are holed.

**Millbank Manor**: one of the few stately houses inside the Red Line, at its inner edge, close to the centre of town and abutting the brick line itself. Not lavish and not audacious, but well kept, in an older neighbourhood that merges gently into the ghetto. A dignified air in a rough part of town.

**The People's Commons** *(name provisional)*: the City's public square, near the base of the Castle but far enough away to keep its own energy. Historically, whatever civic life and organizing the City has had happened here. Close by — a little beyond the pale, given the site — stands a **statue of unity**: to most, a monument; to a Munchkin, a reminder of a crushed rebellion, the bloodshed, and their subjugation.

**The General Market**: the centralized marketplace, near the Tavern and the Commons, none of it far from the Red Line. The cluster draws a genuine cross-section of the whole society — no matter how high-class you are, some rare trinkets, oddities, and artifacts of import are only found in the market, and at a better price than the College of Mages will give you.

**The Whitecourse**, called **the West End** by everyone who does not live there: the aristocratic district, on the west end of town. The name is the pale stone road ringing the Castle, the way red brick rings the Red Line — not too close to the Castle itself (that would be gauche), but easily accessible. Wealth alone doesn't get you in: you can barely rent there without knowing people. Human and elven nobility, very few common or greater Fey — though the few Fey present are a hook into the Seelie Court, which keeps an outpost elsewhere in Squishnestashire. Portal access, where it exists, is in practice reserved for the aristocracy through their College ties — not for Munchkins, agriculturalists, or common folk.

**The Industrial District** *(unnamed)*: a light industrial quarter for abjurative machinery work and what little refining the City does itself.

**The Sewers**: interconnect with the pre-City cave system beneath the region ([The UKaves](../Lore/Questing%20Time!%20Geography.md#the-ukaves)). A locale in their own right — and they may tie to existing factions.

# Denizens

Stat blocks: [Stat Blocks](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md). Abilities: [Abilities](../Mechanics/Abilities.md).

**The Munchkin** - humanoid small people, and a human subspecies carrying a trace of Elf blood too thin to illuminate them. See [Aspects and the Non-Illuminated](../Lore/Magic%20System.md#aspects-and-the-non-illuminated). Indigenous to the agricultural lands around the City, same as Squishnestashire. Many are drawn in by economic opportunity; not everyone who comes escapes poverty. See [The Red Line](#key-locations). [Stats](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#the-munchkin)

**High Elves** - comical parodies of themselves. Long life spans, grace and airs, and a stagnant and decadent culture. [Stats](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#high-elves)

**Humans** - typically farmers and countryfolk. Salt of the earth. Hearty and no nonesense. [Stats](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#humans)

**Fey (Seelie)** - trickster spirits and faeries. [Stats](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#fey-seelie); [Faerie's Grace](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#faeries-grace); [Trickster](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#trickster)

**Dwarves** - the City's industrial folk: train-depot logistics, and what fine industry happens locally — jewelry, weapons, armor, useful artifice. Hold [the Mechanics Guild](../Lore/Factions.md#the-mechanics-guild). [Stats](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#dwarves)

# Factions

Full entries: [Factions](../Lore/Factions.md).

**The College of Mages** - canonizes the Derived Magics. The dominant political faction of the City, open to mages of any race. The building: [The College of Mages](../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md). [Faction](../Lore/Factions.md#the-college-of-mages)

**The founding.** Before New Squishnesta there was **Munchkinland**: the Munchkins' ancestral home, idyllic as they remember it, peaceful and largely ineffectual. Old Munchkins still call the Red Line by the old name — [the Don](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#don-aldo-zuccaro) does it to the party's face. The **College was co-founded first**, by human and elven settlers, before there was a city at all. A local government emerged around it and grew, until it was large enough to found a city, which it called New Squishnesta.

The story the humans tell is harmonious: they came upon Munchkins who were generous with their land, the two civilisations coexisted peacefully, and the humans simply developed faster while the Munchkins stagnated.

**The Homesteaders** - farmers and folk who want to be left alone. [Faction](../Lore/Factions.md#the-homesteaders)

**The Merchants' Guild** - governs the City's legal trade, such as anyone does. [Faction](../Lore/Factions.md#the-merchants-guild)

**The Mechanics Guild** - the Dwarves' guild. [Faction](../Lore/Factions.md#the-mechanics-guild)

**The Five Families** - five crime families running the Red Line's underground trade. [Faction](../Lore/Factions.md#the-five-families)
