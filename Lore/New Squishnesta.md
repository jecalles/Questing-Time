# Overview
Colloquially "The City", a techno-magical metropolis. Fed by abjurative floating traincars filled with the local produce from the surrounding [Squishnestashire](../Lore/Places/Squishnestashire.md), a sparse agrarian community. Economy is mostly agricultural and magical services to other vassal realms. Smells faintly of bubblegum.

Nobody knows what happened to "Old Squishnesta". Some say it never existed. 

The City is young — maybe four generations old. It has no walls: it just fades outward, less and less dense, until it's all pastoral. Population 50,000–100,000, GM leaning 50,000 (Squishnestashire as a whole holds ~300,000 — see [Questing Time! Geography](../Lore/Questing%20Time!%20Geography.md)). On its surface it looks like a near-utopia that governs itself without blood — because the society genuinely works for the people in power. Criminal behavior rarely goes through any legal system: nobody sane crosses [the College of Mages](../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md), and if a minor adept rips you to shreds for trying, that's held to be your own fault. The dynamic isn't tyranny — it's a human not caring what the ant colony next door does. If a prison exists at all, it's the rinky-dink kind you'd expect of a small hamlet (tentative, GM).

# The Trains

The traincars don't actually abnegate gravity — that's a common in-world misunderstanding. Abjurative runes on the underside of the cars and on the tracks repel each other, like positive and negative charges; a gentle push of evocation magic moves the cars along.

**A cargo car is an open stone basin** — curved walls, no roof, and **no doors**. Freight goes in over the side and comes out the same way. So a car is a pit rather than a room: something inside it has total cover from anything shooting past, and no quick way out. Climbing in or out costs a move and an action both. The runes are easy to maintain in place, but sending a field technician out to fix or lay new ones is expensive — rune maintenance is presumed to be a large portion of the economy. The trains are set up for agriculture and goods, not people: [the College of Mages](../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md) benefits most from them politically, and mages can simply teleport via Derived conjuration. Fixed portal sites likely exist, controlled and rent-extracted (tentative, GM). Line layout and its politics: [Trains](../Lore/Questing%20Time!%20Geography.md#trains).

[The Shire](../Lore/Places/Squishnestashire.md)'s agricultural output is mostly monocropping under somewhat austere practices — the surrounding environment pushed hard to subsist — plus a small seafaring trade of minor fishing and whaling off the northern coast.

# Denizens

Stat blocks: [Stat Blocks](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md). Abilities: [Abilities](../Mechanics/Abilities.md).

**The Munchkin** - humanoid small people, and a human subspecies carrying a trace of Elf blood too thin to illuminate them. See [Aspects and the Non-Illuminated](../Lore/Magic%20System.md#aspects-and-the-non-illuminated). Indigenous to the agricultural lands around the City, same as [Squishnestashire](../Lore/Places/Squishnestashire.md). Many are drawn in by economic opportunity; not everyone who comes escapes poverty. See [The Red Line](#key-locations). [Stats](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#the-munchkin)

**Humans** - typically farmers and countryfolk. Salt of the earth. Hearty and no nonesense. [Stats](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#humans)

**Fey (Seelie)** - trickster spirits and faeries. [Stats](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#fey-seelie); [Faerie's Grace](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#faeries-grace); [Trickster](../Mechanics/Abilities.md#trickster)

**Dwarves** - the City's industrial folk: train-depot logistics, and what fine industry happens locally — jewelry, weapons, armor, useful artifice. Hold [the Mechanics Guild](../Lore/Factions.md#the-mechanics-guild). [Stats](../Mechanics/Stat%20Blocks.md#dwarves)

# Factions

Full entries: [Factions](../Lore/Factions.md).

**The College of Mages** - canonizes the Derived Magics. The dominant political faction of the City, open to mages of any race. The building: [The College of Mages](../Lore/The%20College%20of%20Mages.md). [Faction](../Lore/Factions.md#the-college-of-mages)

**The founding.** Before New Squishnesta there was **Munchkinland**: the Munchkins' ancestral home, idyllic as they remember it, peaceful and largely ineffectual. Old Munchkins still call [the Red Line](../Lore/Places/The%20Red%20Line.md) by the old name — [the Don](../NPC%20Sheets/Don%20Aldo%20Zuccaro.md) does it to the party's face. The **College was co-founded first**, by human and elven settlers, before there was a city at all. A local government emerged around it and grew, until it was large enough to found a city, which it called New Squishnesta.

The story the humans tell is harmonious: they came upon Munchkins who were generous with their land, the two civilisations coexisted peacefully, and the humans simply developed faster while the Munchkins stagnated.

**The Homesteaders** - farmers and folk who want to be left alone. [Faction](../Lore/Factions.md#the-homesteaders)

**The Merchants' Guild** - governs the City's legal trade, such as anyone does. [Faction](../Lore/Factions.md#the-merchants-guild)

**The Mechanics Guild** - the Dwarves' guild. [Faction](../Lore/Factions.md#the-mechanics-guild)

**The Five Families** - five crime families running the Red Line's underground trade. [Faction](../Lore/Factions.md#the-five-families)
