Dense forest and bogs only sparsely populated at its perimeter. The name comes from the density and bite of the vegetation, not from the silicon inside. Deep inside, where no one but the Wild ones live, are weird artifacts from a bygone era. Huge silicon monoliths innervated by trees and mycelia hum and glow with pulsating bioenergetic resonance.

# The Logging Site

Somewhere in the Forest, a logging operation was cut short.

**The site is not named or placed yet.** Deliberately left open, to be declared when a session needs it. See Public reputation.

# Factions

Full entries: [Factions](../Lore/Factions.md).

**The Druids of the Forest** - keepers of the Sharp Forest and its denizens. [Faction](../Lore/Factions.md#the-druids-of-the-forest)

**The Unseelie Fey** - Dark Fae of the Unseelie Court, whose own domain lies elsewhere. They know what this land is, they keep it from the Seelie, and they are expanding into the Forest to get at it. [Faction](../Lore/Factions.md#the-unseelie-fey)

**Razor's Edge** - Robyn's people, the **Forest Fae**: a nomadic people who cycle through the Forest with the seasons, named for a cliff-face gathering ground at the far edge of their old range. They keep no court, and are no kin of the Unseelie. [Faction](../Lore/Factions.md#razors-edge)

**The Anachrons** - silicon automata, said to hold the secrets of Chronomancy. [Faction](../Lore/Factions.md#the-anachrons)
